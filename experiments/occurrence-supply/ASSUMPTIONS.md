# What selected occurrence value measures

The invariant counts the committed face value of each selected creation once, up to the first accepted real spend of that creation. A creation has an append position, a commitment and a body `(pk, rho, raw, native, token, effective asset ID)`. A real debit records a private match to a position. Both SQL and the proof verifier remain unchanged. The node stores public commitments, ciphertexts and published nullifiers. It does not store this body, the private match or a decision about the recipient's ability to spend. ## One spend uses one position

`note_gadget.rs:26–103,126–154` and the managed circuit `registry.rs:56–110` implement the following fixed field-word encodings. `r = H(OPEN, asset, token, raw)`, `pk = H(PK, H(AK,ask), nk)`, `cm = H(CM, H(NOTE,pk,rho,r), native)`, and `nf = H(NF,nk,rho,r)`. The actual note tags `AK,PK,NOTE,CM,NF,RHO,NF_DUMMY` are `0x716e0001` through `0x716e0007`. `OPEN` is `0x415354310001`. A digest is four canonical Goldilocks elements. Positive quantities and native values are below `2^62`. [Independent encodings](../funded-membership/reference.py) · [Typed-output builder](../managed-assets/native/profile.rs)

For two allowed openings of the same CM, binding of `CM` fixes the note-inner digest and native value. Binding of `NOTE` fixes `pk,rho,r`. Binding of `OPEN` fixes the token amount, asset tag and raw opening. For two *real* witnesses to that commitment, binding of `PK` fixes `nk` from equality of the two owner digests. The two real-NF evaluations then have identical inputs and hence the same output. This reasoning permits distinct `ask` values that derive the same authorization digest. It requires neither inversion of `PK` at creation nor injectivity of `NF` across all notes. `Binding` supplies collision exclusions only for earlier and later algorithmically encountered preimages in the history being interpreted. The scope of each collision exclusion includes the creation and spend preimages from the locally satisfying witnesses chosen for this finite history. Witnesses from different packets need not be asserted compatible. Equalities between their bodies and real NF preimages are conclusions. A computational collision-resistance reduction must be able to obtain both offending preimages, or state an alternative binding game that covers the joint history. Existential proof soundness alone does not provide such an extractor or a concrete failure probability. [Body binding, same_spend_nf and fresh_nf_implies_unused_position](Occurrence.lean)

The selected ancestor authenticates a **set membership** statement. For a C spend, `circuit.rs:170–219` and `merkle.rs:547–611` check the shared depth, nonzero real root and the selected child at every active level. `consensus.py:15–18,105–111` computes depth from the selected count, at most `4^20` leaves, with a minimum positive depth of one. The chain groups four chronological children and hashes them in sorted digest order. The path's 2-bit position is a position among the presented children. It does not encode the historical SQL leaf index. Header `ap` is the field hash of the normalized preimage reconstructed from the 132-byte header. Its tree root is `header[100:132]`. Under header and tree binding, authentic occupied-path membership places the CM somewhere in the eligible prefix. Unique selected CMs then give precisely one chronological occurrence. The first debit records that occurrence and its published NF. A fresh NF on a later real spend excludes a second debit of the same occurrence by the same-CM argument above. `FundedAnchor.selected_prefix` proves this inference for arbitrary paths when `Path`, the selected-root equation and history-scoped `Binds` hold. `checked_membership` proves it from executable evaluations on a disclosed path. `fromEligibleHeader` takes the selected row and exhibited normalized header, both tied to the same public AP and height, and derives equality of roots under pairwise header binding. `slot_wires` feeds the derived member directly into the transaction and mixed-block induction. ## Comparison with the historical NF inventory

`marked_iff_debited` proves a pointwise result for histories satisfying the older representation's stronger opening conditions. Each birth has a real compatible witness whose NF supplies its label. Distinct positions have distinct labels. An imported historical input-event classification must project to the inherited marks and debits up to permutation. For the C suffix, `selected_classification` and `extend_imported_events` derive both projections from each actual slot flag by induction. The full comparison never requests a precomputed matching for that suffix. `classify_marks` derives the multiset partition into real debit NFs and dummy marks. Dummy marks must avoid the old labels. A real debit's witness can differ from the witness used for labeling. Same-position NF stability identifies their two labels. A mark for another position cannot match by distinctness, and a dummy cannot match by separation. Consequently each funded label is marked exactly when its position has a real debit. `holdings_agree` sums this result for native currency and each asset, allowing the old `settle` order to permute complete notes and marks. `legacy_registry_iff` transfers the earlier outstanding-quantity invariant in either direction. These results take neither equality of the two balances nor equivalence of the per-position death predicates as input. [Agreement proof](Agreement.lean)

For outputs with opening coverage, a source-level route to distinct old labels uses `rho = H(RHO, creator.NF0, creator.NF1, output-slot)`. The selected chain never accepts the same published NF twice, and packet output slots differ. The creation-rho inputs therefore differ across those transaction positions. Collision exclusion for `RHO` and then for distinct real-NF preimages yields different labels. Mere CM uniqueness is insufficient because an NF does not hash the native quantity or owner digest. The old dummy-separation premise also requires binding across the two NF domains. These are sufficient conditions for the comparison. The occurrence invariant does not need distinct NFs across unrelated births or a hypothetical owner's NF at a never-spent output. [Full source-level argument](../supply-refinement/OBLIGATIONS.md)

## Historical boundaries and observations

A block's reward has zero tokens and the selected native subsidy plus fees. Its CM is fresh against both old and same-block outputs. The public reward context binds the network, parent, height, miner key and packet vector. Its rho and raw openings use separate byte domains. Its body supplies an occurrence regardless of whether the miner knows a matching secret for the public reward key. For old-NF comparison, a reward needs compatible opening coverage and collision exclusion between its byte-domain rho encoding and the tagged transaction rho preimages. Under reorganization, selected effects and the private interpretation roll back to the common prefix before branch replay. A detached receipt or prior dummy/real mark does not describe the new head. [Reward reconstruction](../funded-membership/reference.py) · [Prior selected and reorg tests](../funded-supply/README.md)

C activates at height 25. Earlier A/B ancestry and swap legs have separate registered verifier and admission paths. An induction over C supply steps starts from a represented, consistent selected prefix or requires an analogous argument for *every* contributing historical rule. The old `build_constraints` does not gain C's occupied-path rules retroactively. A signed swap output uses the effective raw opening that incorporates its public authorization key before normal typing. A subsequent managed spend uses that effective raw once. Prior managed supply and fixed-asset registration also retain their original IDs, authority and latest revisions. Each of these interfaces matters when a C spend or mint uses older state. [Historical dispatch](../funded-membership/build_c.py) · [Swap output construction](../atomic-swap/native/PROFILE.md) · [Pre-activation and branch boundary](../supply-refinement/OBLIGATIONS.md)

For an imported prefix, supply a faithful chronological birth vector with its exact CMs and committed bodies, an authentic anchor/count projection of that vector, the entire selected public mark set (real and dummy), and a debit book accounting for precisely the real consumptions that occurred there. Each old debit has an allowed authorized witness whose NF is in that mark set. Positions and CMs are unique as required by `HistoryOK`. The starting registry rows are the selected latest rows, with outstanding amounts at most the inclusive ceiling of 2^62−1 and equal to the remaining occurrence sums. This boundary requires a historical real-debit interpretation of the *prefix*, never secrets or NF labels for its unspent births. It is empty at modeled genesis. A subsequent operation outside the four supply actions, ordinary transfers and native rewards (including a swap) needs its own preservation argument or a new justified prefix. Replaying the released H42 prefix publicly validates its consensus history; it does not reveal or certify a complete ghost debit book. No matching for the later C suffix is supplied. [Selected induction and mixed-block scheduling](Occurrence.lean)

A local owner can check a particular receipt by decrypting it, reopening its funded CM, relating it to the selected prefix, and successfully making a real spend. Positive face value can remain in an unspent occurrence even when its intended recipient is permanently unavailable. An accepted public proof authenticates the ciphertext digest but does not link each ciphertext's plaintext to the output body inside the circuit. Even matching digests do not guarantee correct recipient encryption, retention of the right key, delivery or later access to authenticated history. The existential face-value invariant gives no recoverability bound, anonymity theorem, zero-knowledge theorem, delivery guarantee or quantitative post-quantum soundness reduction. A finite wallet/SQL observation only certifies its disclosed history and participating owners. [Receipt and proof boundary](../supply-refinement/OBLIGATIONS.md)

In the author's H48 run, a previously offline owner restored a 32-byte backup and opened one selected receipt containing 19 tokens and 9 native units. An onward payment used one real occurrence and published two marks. It allocated 7 tokens to the payee, 12 as change, 4 native to the payee, 2 native as change and a fee of 3. Three nodes selected the reported chain, and opened holdings totaled the registry's 40. This is positive evidence for receipt recovery with available keys and authenticated history. The offline wallet had generated a valid key at the outset. The observation cannot establish that a freely chosen adversarial recipient digest has a key preimage.
