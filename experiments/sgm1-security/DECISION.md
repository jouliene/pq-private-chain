# Revise SGM1 before pursuing a 128-bit monetary-security claim

**Decision: revise the proof protocol.** Keep profile C on the funded PoW devnet. Keep the exact SGM1 verifier as the executable comparison. A new, isolated ordinary-payment experiment (EAR1) removes one specific extraction obligation at a measured cost of 8,936 bytes per leg. Its present security argument still does not justify funding it on the live chain.

## Why this changes the decision

The prior milestone established that a two-core node could admit genuine SGM1 packets at the 100M/year live comparison load. The outstanding question concerns authorization against a quantum prover who chooses a transaction after observing honest users and interacting with the hash oracle. A concrete partial-oracle decoder now identifies sufficient data for recovering the packed assignment and checking the real funded inputs. When its column table is fixed before the degree challenge, the chance of degree cancellation is at most 2^-267.439 (rounded). A fresh 60-subset can hit only 241 eligible positions with probability at most 2^-256.195. The exact noninteractive SGM1 proof does not give this decoder access to those data at the prefixes where the argument needs them. [Decoder, hypotheses and theorem comparisons](REDUCTION.md) · [Funded-witness implication](MONETARY.md) · [Prior capacity](../sgm1-admission/DECISION.md)

The first missing bridge is a quantum prefix extraction and stability lemma for the actual typed salted Merkle columns, the degree response and Fiat–Shamir states, under adaptive statement selection. Reading a complete classical log of the prover's oracle queries would supply information unavailable in the QROM. The classically emitted intermediate-digest subcase does admit an online quantum extraction result. A noninteractive prover need only emit its final packet. CMS's fixed-output BCS theorem and DFM's generic multi-round reduction each require more than the SGM1 verifier implements or the interactive analysis establishes. The later adaptive IOR theorem supplies a useful review target with a particular vector commitment and compiler. Its premises also require a protocol-level correspondence. No forgery follows from these gaps. [Primary-source analysis](REDUCTION.md)

## Concrete change and cost

EAR1 transmits all 75 rows of 151 two-byte degree-basis evaluations immediately after the degree matrix. They are included in the running transcript before the first circuit fold. The verifier checks each of the 60 authenticated column equations using that same table. This replaces a 64-byte digest and 13,650 bytes of late completion. The old verifier rejects the new proof. EAR1 is a separate registration of the same funded circuit with its own envelope, intent and proof domain. The live network has no EAR1 activation.

## Swap wallet and next decision

Before providing a draft, each owner checks both complete descriptors and peer identity obtained through independently paired endpoints and requires two slot-specific signatures over the same terms. This binds the 64-byte owner and the entire 1,568-byte receiving key to the intended role. The gate precedes receipt sealing and proof creation. The author measured zero draft bytes in 49 denied requests across 15 cases (1,029 control-response bytes). The two successful swaps each contained two 183,360-byte unsigned draft cores. A replay into a new durable ledger reached height 20, 57 outputs and 20 fees and reopened at the same tip. Correct incoming receipt decryption and each final ordered-pair approval remain mandatory. The denial measurements count bytes actually read by the coordinator, including control frames, and the positive run matches each observed draft digest to the settled signed pair. A trusted local launcher authenticates the endpoints for this experiment. A remote deployment would need a separately authenticated enrollment mechanism. [Wallet protocol, tests and evidence](wallet.md)

**Next decision-changing result.** Ask a cryptographic reviewer to construct or refute a typed extractable vector-commitment and transcript correspondence for EAR1 in the adaptive quantum game. It must cover the 4,096 indexed, salted columns, prefix stability under partial openings, variable-width challenge expansion including the degree matrix, the precise ring terminal and the actual interleaving. Deliver either a concrete adaptive extraction inequality for accepted honest-target spends with all query and multi-user losses, or an explicit transformation to a registered compiler with a new wire and runtime budget. A separate simulator must cover receipts and encrypted retries jointly. This is the gate for further funded integration. It does not require repeating capacity tests.

The independent reviewer reproduced the released calculations and both public transcripts, generated a fresh EAR1 payment, tested all wallet denials and matched four new drafts to two settled swaps.
