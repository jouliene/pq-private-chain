# Retain MNG1 and SLG1 on the PoW devnet

**Neither tested wallet-created proof improves assurance at the signed-leg budget.** The CFW construction gives a stronger conditional interactive knowledge-error screen, but its first tree already exceeds the entire proof allowance. The Picnic3 construction removes those Reed–Solomon trees. Its verifier needs too many masked witness and multiplication messages for the funded relation. Keep both registrations, all existing notes and the existing two-owner settlement rule. The reference still lacks an end-to-end 128-bit quantum knowledge and recipient-privacy result.

The experiment starts at `a7aeff63a4bd89da5831723f8610f28b9bd86833`. A transfer consumes two input slots, proves authority and branch membership for its real inputs, derives their nullifiers, creates two funded outputs, privately conserves both asset and native value and charges the native fee. The funded transfer and leg matrices have 136,975 and 140,619 constraints. Their 31 and 34 public words include the ordered receipt digest. The leg additionally binds the ephemeral authorization key. Each wallet verifies that it controls the promised incoming output before signing. The pair authenticates both complete legs, network, terms, expiry and fee payer. The validating node checks current eligibility and spentness and applies both legs atomically. The experiments use this same relation and retain the original receiving suite. ## What failed in CFW

Each side of the graph is `GF(8)^7`. A right vertex `(a,b1,…,b6)` has eight ordered neighbors `(x,b1+a·x,…,b6+a·x^6)`. This construction gives a deterministic distance certificate. With the actual masking rate the first amplified code has guaranteed pairwise agreement at most 0.049106. Checking an arbitrary purported oracle requires a separate bound. Applying CFW Lemma A.1 to its affine fold gives allowed distance 664,723 among 2,097,152 positions. The resulting first-stage query count is 495, costing up to 3,960 underlying evaluations per interleaved row. Each row uses 3,961 seed elements and the unopened coefficients pass into the next switch. Add 4,776 B of outer messages. The whole layout has six roots, 9,903 sibling hashes, 11,754 opened extension elements and a blinded terminal vector of 168 extension elements. Its maximum transfer is 1,175,929 B, its signed leg 1,193,148 B and its signed pair submission 2,386,309 B. These are bounds on the specified honest-verifier query geometry. Bit-reversed indices produce the extremal multiproofs. They also have distinct first-stage graph endpoints. Typical queries also fail the budget. Fitting even the first-stage values requires at least 119 repeat draws among 495 independent uniform samples. A union bound gives probability at most `2^-1044`. In an actual SHAKE256 component probe over 256 public seeds the smallest first-tree opening, including its root, was **696,768 B**. This probe used no prover or wallet. Even granting the amplified tuple code an optimistic Johnson agreement bound gives a whole-proof maximum of 643,112 B. Pairwise distance alone cannot justify that soundness claim. ## What failed in KKW

The alternative uses compressed preprocessing seeds, a hidden MPC party and a broadcast string for each challenged execution. A native build of the pinned Picnic verifier accepted and exactly reserialized its three stock LowMC known-answer specimens. The L5 specimen was 49,024 B for 1,020 AND gates. Its exact serializer charges one hidden-party broadcast bit per AND in each of 68 opened executions plus conditional preprocessing corrections. Merkle compression of the seed cover leaves this term intact. The candidate field gadget computes true Goldilocks multiplication and seventh powers. It agrees with integer arithmetic on boundary inputs and deterministic samples. The full fixed payment and leg expressions contain 18,880 and 19,352 seventh powers. Counting only two schoolbook partial products per input-bit pair gives 1,314,652,160 B and 1,347,518,464 B respectively. [Pinned implementation and reproduction](kkw/README.md) · [Executed arithmetic results](kkw/GATES.json) · [Actual reference verifier results](kkw/KAT.json)

A second calculation tests whether this exclusion depends entirely on a poor multiplier. The third Boolean difference of a full-domain Goldilocks seventh power at the eight lowest inputs is nonzero. A network with at most one AND and any number of XORs and negations has degree at most two. Even at two ANDs per separately implemented full-domain box, the leg requires 328,984 B for online broadcasts. The argument allows a radically different circuit to fuse boxes or prove batched field products. It also leaves open alternate witness encodings that avoid repeating both paths. ## Security and operational consequences

The design target is constant-success forgery or privacy violation at work of at least `2^128` against adaptive wallets and recorders with quantum access to public hash functions. Honest wallet/ledger interfaces remain classical. The CFW calculation gives a conditional interactive exponent of approximately 267.875. At `t=2^128`, the quadratic term in the ideal BCS extraction bound is about `2^-11.875`, and the cubic term at a 512-bit oracle is `2^-128`, before hidden constants, verifier queries and instance composition. Charging a separate query budget to every one of 100M targets changes the crossover. Real adversaries can share work across targets, so that union calculation is a sensitivity, not a calibrated attack cost. CFW's interactive HVZK and CMS's BCS theorem have specific hypotheses. The guarded funded frontend, code-switch timing, seeded masks, direct SHA3/SHAKE coin packets and composition with ciphertexts require their own mapping. The old verifier's 26-query FRI profile and the Picnic signature's parameter label likewise give no 128-bit funded-payment theorem. [Transcript and assumption map](SECURITY.md) · [CFW](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/391) · [CMS Theorem 8.6 and Remark 8.2](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/834)

I reran the released public verifier on the earlier independently generated restored-recipient payment and signed exchange. With three cold launches and five measured checks after warmup in each role, the median warm wall times were **6.37 ms** and **24.55 ms**. Median aggregate CPU was 6.28 and 24.19 ms, with about 201,100 KiB native peak RSS. This replay checks the payment constraints and both complete swap authorizations. The experiment changed no ledger, wallet, asset, mint, burn, signature or consensus code. Both candidate size gates failed before proving, so there are no new funded candidate payments, wallet CPU/RSS measurements, equally warmed candidate verifiers, or new recovery runs. The historical funded wallet and restore measurements remain available. [Raw public replay](LIVE_VERIFY.json) · [Previously measured funded wallets](../funded-whir/DECISION.md)

## Next decision

Test **packed arithmetic threshold MPC-in-the-head** for the registered funded relation. Feneuil and Rivain's TCitH linearized polynomial-constraint construction is a specific starting point. Its batched field checks target both measured obstacles: the chain of separately authenticated CFW RS openings and Picnic's per-Boolean-gate broadcasts. First price all commitments, threshold openings, masked witness material, challenges, repetitions, and terminal checks for 140,619 swap-leg rows. Demonstrate a packed multiplication/check block against the registered matrix oracle using Goldilocks or an explicitly guarded extension. Establish the field, threshold, concrete knowledge and Fiat–Shamir losses before a full prover. Advance only if the complete signed-leg proof budget is at most 241,084 B with two separately signed legs, local spending witnesses and unchanged receipt binding. This is a proposed experiment. The present results do not establish its feasibility. [Arithmetic TCitH constructions](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/1573)
