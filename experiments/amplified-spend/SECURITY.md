# Funded spend assurance at the proposed graph parameters

The adversary can choose statements and keys, observe the ledger, retries and rival forks, interact classically with honest wallets, and retain recorded receipts. For the asymptotic compiler analysis it can also query the hash oracle in superposition. The 128-bit target concerns quantum work at constant success in the specified game. It covers spending knowledge, monetary soundness and privacy separately. Backups and spending keys remain private except in an explicit corruption experiment. A swap opponent learns its own negotiated terms. [Adversary and target](../spend-proof-decision/TARGET.md)

## Where the reductions stop

* **Interactive knowledge.** The incidence-graph distance bound is deterministic. CFW Lemma A.1 covers its affine fold at the stated one-third-distance radius. The inherited EF5 tail estimates additionally assume the indicated scalar-RS list/MCA bounds, round-by-round extraction for the guarded outer R1CS map, and correct composition of the changes in interleave width/alphabet. The conditional summed error is about `2^-267.875`. A classical attacker in the ideal interactive protocol faces those round errors. This number does not establish a concrete classical or quantum exponent for a deployed noninteractive verifier. [Per-stage rates, lists and errors](primary/MODEL.json) · [CFW](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/391)
* **Noninteractive knowledge.** CMS Theorem 8.6 gives extraction probability of order `μ − t²κ − t³/2^λ` under its round-by-round knowledge and random-oracle conditions, with a query surcharge of order `q log ℓ`. Its Section 8.2 alternates a round-index hash and a root update. The funded tape instead absorbs framed tagged messages including plaintext coefficient vectors, derives a digest per verifier-coin packet, then expands and rejection-samples challenges with SHAKE. Trees use salted, labeled multi-input hashing, and overlapping paths are compressed on the wire. A reduction would need to translate these interfaces, the actual allocation of private prover randomness, fresh proof instances, and the adaptively selected statement. CMS Remark 8.2 itself distinguishes hash chains. Fixed-function SHA3/SHAKE instantiation and the hidden constants remain open. [CMS paper](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/834) · [Actual tape](../wallet-created/structured/tape.rs) · [Salted Merkle opening](../wallet-created/structured/oracle.rs)
* **KKW.** The stock Picnic3 C verifier establishes public acceptance and byte layout of published LowMC signatures. The commit-and-open QROM analysis cited in the alternative applies to its specified compiler/games. Replacing the signing relation with our funded relation requires a new statement-bound public relation, a suitable extractor and a concrete multi-instance loss calculation. The package's L5 label gives no funded-wallet bit count. Its byte rejection already holds without assuming a break. [Pinned serializer, quantum source and parameter mapping](kkw/README.md)
* **Monetary integrity and old money.** Extraction must yield the opening and eligible membership for each non-dummy input, enforce the separate zero-value and domain rules for each dummy slot, reproduce the funded nullifiers and satisfy both bounded balance equations. A fixed 256-bit ideal target has generic preimage scales of about 256 classical and 128 quantum query bits. Finding some collision in a freely chosen ideal 256-bit digest instead has generic scales of about 128 and 85.3 respectively. Existing four-limb Poseidon2 commitments are algebraic and have slightly under 256 output bits. These generic numbers are game illustrations. They are neither proven costs for the concrete hash nor bounds on stealing a given coin. Original issuance, funded-note binding, the swap's SHA-256 joint digest and its SLH-DSA-SHAKE-128f signatures retain independent obligations. Previously funded users keep their keys and canonical spent identities. [Funded-note and migration games](../spend-proof-decision/FUNDED_NOTES.md) · [Existing verifier comparison](../spend-proof-decision/DECISION.md)
* **Joint recipient privacy.** Each output includes a 1,792-byte receipt with ML-KEM-1024 encapsulation and ChaCha20-Poly1305 protection under the existing derivation. A private 32-byte backup regenerates the registered receiver's credentials. Structural checks and the digest of both ciphertexts authenticate the bytes. A malicious sender can still encrypt unusable data. Replay of the actual received commitment is the wallet's check. Multi-recipient key privacy, maliciously registered keys, nonce/key derivation, wallet error behavior and the full view including correlated proofs and later spends need one explicit game. Later compromise of a reused receiving secret exposes recorded receipts. The ledger reveals anchor/height, fees, commitments, nullifiers and the paired swap structure, which includes the payment/change slots, signatures, expiry and terms commitment. Endpoint observation, network timing and known counterparty information add leakage.
