# Independent review of the funded proof-size gate

Both specified packet layouts fail the 241,084-byte proof budget before a funded proving campaign. The CFW result follows from the transmitted leaves on an admissible verifier query tape. The Picnic3 result follows from its serialization rule and the Boolean function computed in the funded hash circuit. The bounds below apply to their stated layouts. The independent [integer checks](audit.py) reproduce the [review data](AUDIT.json).

## Incidence graph, agreement and privacy

The two vertex sets each contain `8^7` labels. In the graph's right-to-left-to-right walk, the displacement from `(a,b₁,…,b₆)` is `(h,hx,…,hx⁶)` for each `h,x ∈ GF(8)`. An additive character with coefficient vector `c` diagonalizes this convolution. The sum over `h` is eight exactly when `c₀+c₁x+…+c₆x⁶=0`, and zero otherwise. Thus the eigenvalues of the unnormalized Gram matrix are eight times the numbers of roots. The unique zero character has eigenvalue 64. There are 196 degree-at-most-six characters with six roots, giving the exact second normalized squared singular value `48/64`. The audit counts the entire multiplicity table by inclusion–exclusion on subsets of prescribed roots. The field trace pairing in this argument is nondegenerate.

For a base agreement set of density ε and the set of wholly contained right neighborhoods of density β, the edge covariance is `β(1−ε)`. Applying the second singular value to the centered indicators and squaring gives `β≤3ε/(4−ε)`. At degree 135,098, ε is at most `135098/2097152`. This distance guarantee compares codewords. For the registered affine fold, CFW Lemma A.1 separately allows Hamming radius 664,723 with `3D<(1−β)N`; its exception numerator is `D+1`. The list there has at most one codeword since `2D` is below the distance. The resulting miss bound and seed allocation solve to 495 queries. The full Johnson radius established for plain RS with scalar positions cannot simply be transferred to the incidence tuples. The published 1.5 Johnson result and tightness examples concern general linear codes. Stronger structure specific to these tuples would need a separate argument. [CFW, §13 and Lemma A.1](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/391) · [Bordage et al., Tables 2–3](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.CCC.2026.24) · [Kominers, Thaler and Zheng](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/235/)

Take the 21-bit reversals of the first 495 nonnegative integers. Every slope is zero, so their neighborhoods `(x,b)` are disjoint. This opens **3,960 distinct base evaluations per row**. The two interleaves transmit 316,800 bytes of extension-field elements, before salts or hashes. Each row uses 3,961 private seed elements, including the spare. Distinct-point evaluations of the low seed coefficients give a full-row-rank Vandermonde matrix for these reads. Charging only one base point per graph query would invalidate this masking argument. Conditional simulation still has to cover the initial blinded guards, the folded rows and OOD response with fresh nonzero pad weights, and the last independent masked codeword. The terminal sends one masked evaluation of the form and 168 blinded coefficients, then opens both prior commitments at the same positions. The stage-specific direct terms, two terminal salts per opened leaf pair and both authentication frontiers are priced. CFW's interactive simulator and the inherited proof statement alone do not supply a quantum joint-view simulator for the two receipts, repeated nullifiers or malicious counterparties. A receiving wallet must authenticate the intended amount, asset and spend authority on the actual output before authorizing the pair. ## Which size claim survives stronger agreement bounds?

For 495 dispersed positions in a height-21 binary tree, the 5,957 sibling digests give a 729,992-byte first stage after roots, values, salts and direct messages. Counting all five stages gives a **1,172,088-byte maximum proof**, or 1,193,148 bytes in one signed leg. The bit-reversal query family reaches the combinatorial maximum for the underlying verifier coin model. An attainable tape is sufficient to exclude a uniform size guarantee. It does not establish that every Fiat–Shamir transcript realizes that tape. Duplicate positions reduce actual serialization. Even a perfect-distance code used at the one-third radius needs 438 queries for the 256-bit miss screen, costing 280,320 value bytes on distinct leaves of width two and degree eight. The paper's generic distance claim has no universal packet-size implication. [Registered wire model](../primary/MODEL.json)

Completely remove the first stage from the bill. Retain the registered four plain RS stages and their 272-bit per-stage target, and ignore all masks, direct messages, slack and auxiliary coefficients. Basic message lengths are 8,192, 512, 32 and 32. Even with optimistic Johnson agreement `sqrt(main/N)` the required counts are at least 91, 68, 50 and 46. Dispersed positions then require at least **376,608 bytes** for the four stages' salted trees, including both at the terminal. This independent lower bound on the worst-case footprint still exceeds the whole leg budget. Improving only the incidence-stage theorem cannot reverse the result. A change to later commitments, folds or their physical opening codec deserves its own evaluation.

The reported 267.875 interactive bits are conditional round-error accounting. Their ideal single-instance quadratic-loss crossover is about 133.94 bits. The exact noninteractive transcript, hash/preimage games for the old notes, multi-target work factor and receipt privacy remain assurance obligations. The previous funded proofs exercise different registered parameters. No new funded proof or PoW inclusion follows from the projections here. [CMS Theorem 8.6](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/834) · [Funded proof decision](../../funded-whir/DECISION.md)

## Picnic3 compression and funded Boolean function

The L5 profile has 601 preprocessing runs, 16 virtual parties and 68 online challenges. Opened parties' input tapes and messages can be recomputed. The outgoing response contains the hidden party's message in each of the 68 online transcripts, exactly `68·ceil(G/8)` bytes for `G` AND gates. Corrections for the last party when it is opened, masked inputs, seeds, commitments and delimiters are additional terms. The stock 49,024-byte L5 specimen has 1,020 AND gates and 8,704 bytes of hidden-party broadcasts. It is a LowMC component test. [KKW construction](https://eprint.iacr.org/2018/475) · [Pinned Picnic3 serializer](https://github.com/microsoft/Picnic/blob/c56abd3baf6629c1f1c122929ebbaac8a9baec15/picnic3_impl.c#L1001-L1054)

In the explicit network, each seventh power includes two nonsquare 64×64 schoolbook products. Their raw partial products alone cost 8,192 ANDs per box. Four boxes give 278,528 response bytes. Multiplying by the actual frontend's 18,880 ordinary or 19,352 leg boxes gives respective partial response floors of 1,314,652,160 and 1,347,518,464 bytes. Arithmetic reduction, modular addition, other witness checks and preprocessing are still unpaid.

A second test removes the schoolbook assumption. On the eight canonical inputs varying only the low three bits, the XOR of `x⁷ mod p` is `0x9ac50`. At least one output bit therefore has Boolean algebraic degree at least three. An XOR/NOT circuit with at most one AND has degree at most two. Any circuit that separately implements each full-domain box needs at least two ANDs per invocation, even with the best modular multiplication gadget. Under the same broadcast format this gives **320,960 bytes for an ordinary transfer and 328,984 for one leg**. This argument permits arbitrary linear wiring inside each box. Cross-box optimization, witness checks that batch field multiplications, or a different MPC protocol fall outside its scope.
