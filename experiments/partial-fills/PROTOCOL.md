# One off-chain order, several independently settled swaps

An order fixes a random private ID, network, full maker receiving descriptor, sell asset, buy asset, total, base-unit lot, integer price per lot and native fee. For a fill of q units the maker requires q > 0, q divisible by the lot, q no larger than the remaining available capacity, and the consideration exactly (q / lot) × price within the amount range. At 10 A for 1 B, a 50 A fill pays 5 B plus the separate 3 native units funded by the buyer. The node sees an ordinary SWB3 atomic swap on the same funded C network. Offers, outstanding quantities, negotiations and reservations have no consensus encoding.

## Authorization

The private firm reply binds the order and advertisement, request ID, capability hash, both wallets' full receiving addresses, both assets, quantity, lot price, consideration, fee payer, height deadline, wall-clock validity and fresh nonce. Its canonical encoding binds the private swap terms through their blind and commitment. The parties build their independently funded SLG3 legs in separate wallet roles. The underlying registered C wallet checks the peer leg, decrypts its incoming output under its own key and checks the exact asset and value. Before signing it checks both funded proofs, its outgoing leg, own change, current eligibility and spending pins, its derived swap key, and its own fee. Each signature binds the ordered pair under the C network, terms commitment, expiry and payer. The maker additionally checks the buyer signature. The node applies both adjacent SWF3 frames with a single native fee and verifies current nullifiers under the existing chain rules. ## Reservation and branch semantics

The private wallet lock serializes maker requests and recovery. SQLite serializes quantity updates and commits them durably. An unsigned reservation can expire or be cancelled. Before the maker invokes its native signer, it durably pins the exact pair, buyer signature, buyer descriptor, economics and selected view. The pin prevents allocation of this quantity to another buyer. A crash between the pin and the native signer can leave an uncertain exposure. Closing the order prevents a new native signature even for a pre-existing pin. The maker can still recover a signature already released by the native wallet. The maker stores the exact fully signed packet before sending the reply. The private native wallet keeps its independent input and signing journal. Power-loss behavior assumes normal local SQLite and filesystem durability guarantees. The executable crash controls use process exits and restarts. [Maker accounting](../stranger-swap/partial_state.py) · [Crash controls](../stranger-swap/partial_state_check.py)

At a selected tip, `remaining = total − confirmed`. On an open order, `available = remaining − reserved − exposed`. Reserved counts revocable unsigned fills. Exposed counts uncertain pins and released pairs outside the selected branch. A closed order has zero available, even when some of its total never sold. Confirmation requires the exact signed SWB3 identity reconstructed from both adjacent frames in the same validated block. A mempool acknowledgment does not count. A reorg removes detached confirmations and restores the same authorizations to exposure. A later canonical inclusion of those same bytes settles them once. Passing an expiry height can be reversed by a deeper fork. The maker conservatively continues charging its signed exposure even above expiry. This can tie up capacity indefinitely for an abandoned signed trade. Manually discarding wallet state or duplicating a maker from an old backup would invalidate the honest-maker promise.

The wallet trusts its own full node to select and validate the chain. Its market cache indexes immutable public blocks from the selected tip back to the same genesis and switches ancestry on a reorg. Ordinary market reconciliation fetches previously unseen blocks using common block IDs, independently of wallet ownership. The buyer's ambiguous-delivery recovery additionally reads public swap-containing blocks to find both exact signed legs and its own signature, verifies the recovered counter-signature and packet, and rechecks the selected tip. This local-chain fallback can read substantial history, and needs access to those bodies. The market cache grows with the observed history and forks. Consensus, core wallet witness updates, and selection of eligible funding anchors retain the funded deployment's rules. ## Visibility

| Observer | Information available |
|---|---|
| A directory acting only as a relay | A generic private-swap service, network, short-lived signed identity, certificate fingerprint, maker endpoint, registration, expiry, clients listing the directory and timing. |
| Anyone connecting to the advertised maker | The private inspection request is open to strangers. The maker returns a stable order ID, both assets, lot price, total, available quantity, closure state and fee. A scraper, including a relay acting as a client, can poll inventories and correlate offers across advertisement changes. |
| Maker and buyer | The counterparty's full receiving descriptor, requested economics, quote and expiry, each negotiated proof, affected public nullifiers and the signed pair. The maker sees competing requests and can link repeated interactions. Each wallet keeps its own spending secrets and private membership paths. |
| Network or forwarding operator | IP endpoints, connection timing, directions and encrypted lengths. The registered TLS link uses X25519 + ML-KEM-768 and application-level ML-DSA-65 authentication of the channel exporter, with a classical TLS certificate. Size and timing can distinguish a final authorization exchange. |
| Chain-only observer | Each recognizable pair of ordered frames, roles, native fee/payer, height deadline, anchor, blinded terms commitment, nullifiers, output commitments, ciphertexts and signatures. An identical retry or re-inclusion is linkable. Different fills have fresh terms commitments and no common public order ID. Timing and information from market participants can still correlate them. |

This is an online honest-wallet allocation policy and real atomic settlement on a research devnet. A maker can withhold service, post overlapping or misleading advertisements, or decline a request. Polling and reserving can tie up liquidity. Network anonymity, joint proof-and-receipt privacy and 128-bit post-quantum monetary assurance remain open. The EAR1 and EC01 research branches stay parked.
