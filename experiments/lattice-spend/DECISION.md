# Which architecture should get the next funded prototype?

**Keep MNG1 transfers and SLG1 swaps spendable. Select the fixed-arity four-child parent with a branch-derived auxiliary root and degree-seven TCitH for the next bounded funded experiment.** The strongest reason to spend engineering effort on that experiment is the reduction in wallet commitment work on the same complete relation. At the previous 2²⁰-party geometry it also saves 28,168 bytes per signed leg. Crossing the deployed wire-size or security benchmark remains a separate decision. Neither lattice route in this study earns a funded integration run.

The adversary of interest controls wallets and peers, chooses statements adaptively across branches, sees all public transaction bytes and can make quantum oracle queries. The research target is a 128-bit quantum work factor against producing an accepted spend without an authorized witness, including a false conservation statement. The required knowledge argument must relate the success of a quantum prover to extraction of a witness for that exact accepted statement, with concrete query and multi-target losses. Knowledge of openings, computational binding of the note and branch, and hiding of the proof jointly with receipts and retries require separate arguments. The statement includes both private inputs, full 2⁴⁰-output capacity, nullifiers, asset/native conservation, fees, ordered outputs and ciphertext binding. Swap legs remain separately controlled and jointly authorized.

**SIS.** The two hidden paths must open exactly the note commitments calculated by the monetary circuit from each spend secret. Two independent membership proofs for arbitrary hidden leaves would not prevent inflation. The specified randomized link must enforce equal canonical limb bits, authorized root and count, real/dummy treatment, original nullifiers and identical bridge statement at both verifiers. Even before this link, at the authors' illustrative n=q=256 and depth 40 each proof sends 815,104 bytes of masked values per response. Two paths and 438 independent ternary repetitions cost 751,433,676–1,094,484,036 B for the specified rank codec before linkage. The 438 count prices an interactive error target of 2⁻²⁵⁶. A 128-bit quantum knowledge bound would require additional results. The SIS reduction for the accumulator alone and the prospect of fewer TCitH shares do not offset this cost under the tested split. This result leaves other accumulator proofs open. [Design, byte inventory and controls](sis/README.md)

**LaZer.** We compared the authors' advertised source pin, corrected C submodule and later public work on recursive extraction, ran the actual hiding membership prover and constructed a verifier with no private path input. The unmodified published Table 3 function at the paper’s exact revision accepted its one-path depth-64 proof. On the instruction emulator its prover took 245.87 s and its verifier 95.45 s. The clean reviewer also generated and verified a new canonical cross-process depth-64 frame of 136,729 B. The published table covers a lattice-native membership relation. [Paper-pin measurement](lazer/PAPER.json) · [Public reproduction](review/CLEAN.md) Our two-path component adds a common supplied public root; a full funded candidate instead needs every original Goldilocks row. In our direct lowering it requires over 1.18 million length-one binary polynomial vectors. We ran the actual parameter chooser, counted the terminal witness, all public fields, receipts and signatures, and derived source-level lower bounds for both parties. All four funded wallet roles passed the full integer conversion on their own fresh released assignments, including the restored onward spend. The patched genuine two-path depth-40 membership proof verified in a new process using 136,741 B including its 512-byte root and descriptor framing. This cannot be substituted for a funded payment proof. On an illustrative 16 GiB home machine the signed-leg verifier floor alone consumes 81.3% of memory and the wallet floor exceeds physical memory. Sequential verification of two legs can reuse a working set. This machine profile is an engineering comparison, not an owner-imposed limit. Even an optimally compressed packet would leave these implementation costs in place. The reference wallet measured about 4.69–4.83 s elapsed and around 452,000 KiB for the released proof IPC; warm public verification was about 6.5 ms for an ordinary transfer and 25 ms for a signed pair on the comparison host. A payment-level LaZer proving time, verifier time and measured peak RSS are unknown. We did not allocate resources to generating a full proof using a layout whose first-stage payload alone is this large and which supplies no established quantum-knowledge improvement. This is an engineering decision about this lowering. **The change that makes further work worthwhile.** Each legacy four-child parent absorbs sixteen Goldilocks limbs and incurs a third permutation for length padding. A fixed-arity, separately tagged domain uses two permutations while preserving the original 20 levels, both input slots and all monetary gates. At the same 2²⁰ parties the signed frame falls from 259,964 to 231,796 B, inclusive of the new root. At the new first fitting 2¹⁸ it is 261,068 B, 1,104 B larger than the former first-fit frame and 100,622 B above the live one. That choice processes 1,117,782,016 B of field shares per leg instead of 6,241,124,352 B (−82.09%). These are bytes streamed into wallet commitments, distinct from RAM or network traffic. At 2¹⁶ the complete signed frame still needs 310,220 B. No full new TCitH signature or end-to-end cost is inferred from arithmetic satisfaction. **Network cost.** At 100 million submitted and included operations, with 90% ordinary and 10% swaps, four body slots per block and the published four-byte slot lengths, the packed annual archive scenarios are 16.106435 TB live, 23.696655 TB for the toolkit raw projection and 26.714935 TB for the proposed pivot. ## Security and compatibility

* The toolkit's revised LNP-Lite result and interactive reductions rely on Module-SIS/Module-LWE bounds, norm growth, rejection sampling and a two-secret extended module assumption. The Appendix C proof is for the unstructured problem. Neither its heuristic classical Fiat–Shamir discussion nor a 128-bit challenge seed gives this full protocol a 128-bit quantum knowledge extractor. The later LEASH interactive analysis requires different projection and extraction conditions and leaves the optimized terminal and noninteractive transform outside its scope. Source-pinned differences, including ordinary JL geometry and the packet's statement construction, remain significant. [Reviewed source/theorem map](review/REVIEW.md) · [Revised toolkit](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1289) · [LEASH](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2898)
* The pivot inherits the preceding packed protocol's multi-round Fiat–Shamir and knowledge assumptions. The additional exact fixed-length Poseidon2 domain needs an independent capacity/separation and multi-target collision/second-preimage analysis. Generic collision search for a 256-bit output has a different query scale from a targeted second preimage against an already funded ledger root. Neither alone demonstrates theft or the targeted end-to-end bound. Live MNG1/SLG1 remains an executable reference with open security assumptions. [Earlier extraction review](../packed-tcith/review/REVIEW.md) · [New hash specification](pivot/README.md)
* Receivers retain the existing two 1,792 B ML-KEM-1024 and ChaCha20-Poly1305 receipts per proof. Before signing, each swap owner must decrypt its incoming receipt, check its asset, amount and spend key, and agree to both complete frames. Public positions of swap outputs, fee, anchor, count, joint authorization, retries and repeated nullifiers on competing branches remain observable. A proof simulator and an IND-CCA KEM claim in separate games do not prove composable recipient anonymity, key privacy, witness hiding with correlated ciphertexts or post-compromise privacy after seed disclosure. [Exact receiving suite and limitations](../spend-proof-decision/RECEIVING.md)
* A candidate upgrade must leave the original note commitment, spend key, branch policy, occurrence order and nullifier derivation intact. Build the auxiliary root from the same canonical commitments and verify it against the selected branch independently of the claimant. Maintain one shared spent set and enforce atomic conflict checks across proof versions. Only accept versioned, recomputed roots after registration; reorgs roll back both trees and the same spent identities. A seed-restoring wallet scans the same ciphertexts and builds the new witness locally. Existing funds remain usable via the original verifier. In all four one-seed roles, the complete fixed-parent circuit retained the actual funded public effects and nullifiers; the restored role consumed the original counterparty output. A local rule check refused the second use of the same nullifiers in both registration orders without changing the spent set. The deployed chain continues to use its original registrations. ## Next funded experiment and its stopping condition

Freeze the branch-derived auxiliary-root registration, reviewed fixed-arity hash domain and canonical wiring. Construct one complete 2¹⁸-party signed leg against a real selected funded occurrence in a disposable network. Verify all three Fiat–Shamir challenge stages and both monetary broadcasts in a new public process with the verifier selecting the root, then try the same nullifier through the old registration and a stale root after reorganization. Measure wallet peak RSS/CPU, signed bytes and warm verifier CPU together. If the security review cannot justify the new domain or the full transcript misses its 1,076-byte headroom, keep the live path and reprice a change to note arithmetic itself. Progress from that first funded leg to a fresh transfer, a jointly authorized swap and a recovered onward spend only on passing the gate. A distinct lattice follow-up would require packed/streaming ring-native constraints and a concrete extractor; substituting a faster entropy coder cannot reduce the public statement working set. Global permissionless PoW development remains open.
