# Independent stranger-swap review plan

Starting revision `ccf62bf7b271f49f96efdc0cc49cab19e972fb44`. An independent reviewer will use an anonymously fetched candidate with a new chain, newly funded owners and a fresh seller restore. One proof slot belongs to the orchestrator until assigned. The necessary evidence spans three different channels.

| Observation | Independent test |
| --- | --- |
| Public discovery | Observe a new client reach the advertised makers with one configured relay unavailable. Parse actual published advertisement frames; enforce their declared public schema, bounds, lifetimes, deduplication, authentication and broad service filtering. Exact market terms are private. Compare their fields with the private artifacts locally and publish only test results. |
| Private firm quotes | Verify two distinct advertised maker identities and private replies delivered on their separately authenticated TLS 1.3 connections. Check request binding, exact settlement currencies and quantities, fee side, expiry and recipient instructions. The firm quote is authenticated by the channel. Distinguish indicative public information from the private executable quote and from a completed joint authorization. Confirm that transferring one maker's reply to another session fails before any signature or submission. |
| Channel authentication | Record the parties' actual negotiated TLS version, group and peer identities where supported. Exercise the intended peer verification, changed binding and refused lower-security negotiation through the shipped interfaces. Distinguish relay outage from peer authentication failure. |
| Abandoned or disconnected negotiation | Count accepted and rejected submission calls directly where instrumented, and compare with the ordinary node's pending set, selected tip, spent set, cumulative fees and archived transaction identities. Record when the disconnect happened relative to release of each final authorization. A completed pair may be submitted by its holder after disconnection. |
| Actual settlement | Parse both complete physical frames, derive their joint digest independently, verify both previous registered leg proofs, two final signatures and receipt digests, and reconstruct the containing PoW block, fee and miner credit. Check end-to-end that the seller spends one received 8-native output on 5 native plus the ordinary fee of 3 after restoring from seed. Participant-local row checks must bind that input to the actual published commitment and spending nullifier. |
| Traffic and observation scope | Count plaintext through each protocol framing layer and TLS transport bytes including handshakes, separately per connection and direction. Avoid adding both ends of one stream as separate network copies. Keep discovery, request/reply, successful or aborted negotiation and the existing two-frame devnet submission separate. State which IP/TCP retransmissions, headers, handshakes and node gossip are directly measured, inferred or excluded. |
| Evidence | Allowlist public ads, public registry keys, on-chain packets and sanitized results. Keep address material, private quotes and negotiations, certificates' private keys, backups, wallet databases, openings, seeds and unredacted TLS transcripts outside release archives. Use the protocol's disclosed application parameters when interpreting who can correlate identities, terms, timing and volume. |

This tests actual isolation, authentication and funded monetary effects under the specified implementation. A local simulation, separate namespaces on one physical machine and independent hosts are distinct deployments and must be labelled accordingly. Quantum assurance for the previous spend and receipt composition remains a separate open research question.
