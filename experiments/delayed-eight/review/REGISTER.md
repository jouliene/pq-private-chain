# Independent review registration for delayed DLF1 certification

Baseline `d9e4114967302916354a7f75787ada2b5e2d27fe`. Registered before the new certificate or network results. The comparison counts 100M included two-input/two-output payments in 365 days. Archive figures refer to one copy of selected authenticated history. Link traffic, losing forks, hot original authorizations, replicas, serving indexes, wallet recovery and backups have separate owners.

## Decision gates

| Gate | Observation required |
| --- | --- |
| Source/authorization | Independently derive the registered outer verifier from the fixed inner DLF1 verifier. Verify eight distinct genuine wallet authorizations in a public-only process and match each slot to its authorized anchor, height, two nullifiers, two commitments, fee and digest of both exact ordered receipts. Reject malformed proof, substituted receipt or wrong verifier. Distinguish eight useful original statements from padded or reused slots. |
| Block placement | From a pinned empty genesis or a fully validated old prefix, recompute each PoW ID, work and parent, ordered statement commitments, inclusion-height/anchor rule, subsidy plus exact fees, commitment root and current uniqueness checks. Original authorization age is evaluated at its block of inclusion. Changing proof representation must preserve the identity of every selected block and its reward opening. |
| Fallback/direct service | Independent senders submit complete direct authorizations while the optional builder is absent, offline or behind. Charge original wallet upload and relay. A later certificate cannot undo a valid admission or make overdue submissions eligible. An already proved singleton remains a valid way to fill a missing statement if it is available. |
| Clean compacted replay | Remove original authorization files from the input view, then use an empty isolated verifier with only public source, approved verification parameters, genesis, public PoW input and approved compact evidence. Compare selected contents, per-height supply/fees and funded/spent state with a fresh direct replay. Preserve exact ciphertexts and commitment positions for seed-only recovery; test real spending when the milestone claims that capability. |
| Reorganization and gaps | Include a heavier competing branch with genuine conflicts or a subset of covered slots. Materialize the winning state from the common ancestor and check unused slots remain inert. A high-work header/statement with no covering authorization cannot advance fully validated state. Supply missing evidence later and verify recovery, or document the resulting availability failure. Enforce original anchor/economic checks on the replacement branch. |
| Retention | Record which node holds each original, certificate, receipt and core before/after compaction, the horizon and additional replicas needed for independent bootstrap or deep rollback. Digest-only commitments cannot regenerate missing material. Verify later reads after deleting canonical originals. Charge any required fallback provider. Distinguish a storage experiment from crash-safe node-integrated compaction and peer retrieval. |
| Service and security | Report fresh setup, warm construction and public verification CPU, elapsed time and peak RSS, plus actual byte counts and finite arrival/queue observations. Fixed-profile FRI query/rate screens leave concrete NI/QROM, knowledge and transcript assumptions. Public-only wrapping can expose grouping and submission identities; a hiding DLF1 inner proof already carries the wallet secrecy obligation. Audit every verifier's code/config binding and any dummy policy. |

For public replay, reproduce direct-only, certificate-only and missing-coverage views without a database of prior decisions. Also compare a historical certificate whose proof remains valid but whose slot is ineligible at a different height/branch. Check held-out source IDs and arbitrary ordering/substitution through normal public acceptance. Keep monetary amounts and input provenance established by wallet instrumentation separate from the public stake/spent sets.

## Arithmetic before native measurement

A year has 31,536,000 s. The mean arrival rate is 3.170979 inclusions/s. Perfect eight-way packing needs 12.5M certificates/year, or one every **2.52288 s**. A warm construction using `C` CPU-s and `W` wall-s requires `0.3963724 C` average cores network-wide and at least `ceil(W / 2.52288)` equally performing serial workers at 100% utilization. Actual queuing needs headroom and independently admitted data. A two-core or four-core builder allocation therefore supplies only 5.04576 or 10.09152 CPU-s per group of eight. These are hardware scenarios and do not limit unilateral direct inclusion.

The baseline assumptions are a length-framed 226 B core plus 32 B per selected statement, two 1792 B receipts per payment and 13 B certificate framing. Each selected core includes reward/nonce. The sparse mix averages 200/121 blocks per inclusion including empties, the busy mix 20/63. Full eight-slot proof-only ceilings are 48,070/50,485 B at 1 decimal TB per year and 368,070/370,485 B at 5 decimal TB. Validate the real encoding, including any certificate-only or per-slot metadata outside the proof. Unused slots and duplicate receipts debit the actual archive. A certificate of `P` proof bytes that carries all eight receipt pairs but covers `u` useful distinct placements costs `(P + 13 + 8 × 3584)/u` B per useful placement before cores and statement IDs. At `u=4`, sparse/busy five-TB ceilings fall to 169,692/170,900 proof bytes if the other four pairs never cover another placement. A later use can amortize previously unused slots, subject to the retained branch and eligibility rules.

A complete DLF1 payer sends 143,187 B before transport. At the 100M rate, one day of unavailable background construction accumulates about **39.23 GB** of original authorization packet bytes on each retaining copy. Retention from zero to the first ready certificate includes time to fill the first group, outstanding work, and any chosen finality/reorg delay. Active wallet proof generation, initial admission, original relay, optional builder work, compact evidence distribution, retained alternate branches and durable source availability all consume resources independently. A fully occupied eight-slot proof passing a size threshold does not establish sustainable service or availability after withheld data.

The selected DLF1 wire, computation and privacy checks were independently tested at the baseline. Source-only proposals, stored hashes, replayed old proof bytes and synthetic pacing cannot establish the new eight-way circuit.
