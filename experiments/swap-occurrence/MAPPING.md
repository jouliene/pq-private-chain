## Premises carried across the boundary

* A faithful inherited prefix gives the chronological CMs and committed bodies, the real debit book for exactly its past consumed positions, all selected public marks (including dummies), authentic ancestor roots/counts, and consistent registry rows. The H33 fixture contains earlier A/B history. A public replay verifies its rules but cannot independently disclose its hidden input links. No output owner secret or hypothetical future NF is required at birth. There is no additional consistency checkpoint following a covered C swap. [Inherited premises](../occurrence-supply/ASSUMPTIONS.md)
* Soundness for the exact accepted SLG3/MNG3 statements is required to interpret them as local satisfying witnesses. For a general computational binding argument across maliciously chosen proofs one needs an extraction/composition argument, or an appropriately scoped binding game. The required bindings concern actual pairs of header, Merkle-parent, CM, NOTE, OPEN and owner-PK preimages. Determinism gives the same NF for the same authorized occurrence. Phase 1C's managed issuance preflight, issuer authorization and ID-binding assumptions continue to apply on the corresponding steps. [Previous packet refinement](../supply-refinement/OBLIGATIONS.md)
* The interpretation of deployed rows assumes the source's canonical field/byte encodings, selected-ledger and transaction semantics, and use of the pinned worker. Neither the kernel audit nor correct replay yields a concrete 128-bit quantum reduction. Two signatures authenticate the common ordered bytes. Ciphertext digest equality alone does not prove correct recipient decryption; knowledge of the receiving secret, honest construction and history availability matter. Joint privacy of the receipts, proofs and retries remains open. [Receipt boundary](../occurrence-supply/ASSUMPTIONS.md)
