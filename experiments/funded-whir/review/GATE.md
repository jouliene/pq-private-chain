## Opening, knowledge and privacy conditions

1. The verifier owns the matrix, wire domains and the 31 ordinary or 34 swap public-field sequence. Check a new witness against the old native verifier's projections, including all four digest limbs of the ordered receipt pair and all eight 32-bit ephemeral key words for a leg. An ordinary statement has fee 3 and five zero operation fields. A leg has fee 0 or 3, zero supply transition and a real first input. Recompute the ordered receipts' digest from the ciphertext bytes outside the arithmetic proof. Bind all public fields, suite, matrix digest and context before deriving any challenge. Require membership of each real input note under the selected funded anchor, fresh nullifiers, canonical nonzero distinct output commitments, and independently verified fresh signatures on both complete ordered legs. New outputs acquire membership when the block containing them is accepted. No receipt-only proof or second independent witness supplies the spending authority.
2. The prover commits its interleaved oracle before the four guard challenges. Use independent base-field guard blinds at distinct fixed coordinates. Each masked evaluation must cover every private source and authenticate to that same committed vector. A nonzero nonbase coordinate gives a base-variable polynomial in the evaluation point; each independent guard costs at most degree/p per decoded candidate. A blind constant may be nonbase for a dishonest prover and cannot cancel a nonconstant source term. Then apply the list factor once and include the final random batching error. Verify canonical field elements, every Boolean selection/range, the domain separation of dummy nullifiers and both bounded integer conservation laws.
3. For the honest interactive view, condition first on fixed public statement, witness, guard coins and guard answers. The four independent uniform base blinds absorb their replies. The separate extension masks for the three final A/B/C values require a last outer challenge outside {0,1}. Include the masked cube sum, all 19 consistent six-coefficient outer messages, and the entire batch. For each inner fold account for the 10 fresh extension mask coordinates, masked sum, four three-coefficient polynomials, correlated opened rows and independent RS seeds. The code switches have fresh OOD pads of 16, 2 and 1 words; their linear coefficient vector must be nonzero. Their opening simulation is conditional on the prior query view.
