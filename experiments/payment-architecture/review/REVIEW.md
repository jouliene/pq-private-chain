# Independent acceptance checks

Registered against the three source designs before the direct spend circuit was built. The released reference for public state and offline receipt behavior is `6502ebe7c01e174bcc8b9718130d7cb5f5dd8e63`. Review covers the following questions.

| MatRiCT+ | Source-bound native authorization for two genuinely owned input accounts, public verifier, public ring of eligible funded pairs, two outputs, complete serial/amount/fee relation, stable conflict detection across refreshed rings. Count indices, output accounts, receiving data and all framing. Test repeated funding by independent offline senders from the same address file. | An actual recipient key printed as an output links a payment to anyone holding that address. A finite public list of keys has the same membership test. Serial reuse on separate genuine coins or inability to carry an authenticated native fee disqualifies this integration. Record scope of ROM proof and demonstrated ring anonymity set. |

Its validity alone cannot authenticate their inclusion in particular PoW blocks. Charge original traffic and temporary retention. | Direct hiding leaf | Decode the registered verifier profile, degree, public input count, active row blinding, and salted witness-dependent commitments. Inspect the actual prover randomness, public verifier input and canonical parser. Distinguish public acceptance, malicious-sender delivery and adaptive PQ anonymity. Both receipt digests must bind exact bytes on submission. No unblinded witness proof may leave its payer. |

For every candidate price the full singleton, low and high occupancy, empty blocks and irregular partial groups. Report 100M included payments/year as the comparison denominator, and distinguish offered payments, retries and original direct uploads. Annual comparison points are 10,000 and 50,000 bytes per inclusion. Keep full raw history, compact authorization history, pruned live state and seed recovery history separate. Conditional storage deletion requires a verifiable chain of new commitments, available openings or public recovery data, and a stated rollback policy. Timing of a successful batch does not establish an always-available builder or a stationary service rate.

For private assets an owner/supply rule must bind asset identifiers to commitments, constrain conservation per asset with bounded integer arithmetic, and charge fees in the native asset. For a swap both owners authorize the same complete ordered exchange, fees, version, anchor/expiry and both sets of outputs. Settlement must atomically check conflicts for both owners. Two separately mined valid sends allow one-sided settlement. Test owner abort, timeout, conflicting inputs, rollback, seed discovery across assets and unilateral availability. A design extension is a separate conclusion until there are separate wallets and a real integrated proof.

The fork's 26×5 = 130 query/rate screen and its roughly 128-bit Goldilocks quadratic extension are only constructor limits. The actual FRI bound needs a proximity/knowledge analysis for this finite profile, challenge sampling and batching, transcript soundness, and a reduction for noninteractive quantum queries. Poseidon-Goldilocks inside the proof system and the QROM reduction remain separate obligations. Four ~64-bit hash words give an ideal generic quantum collision-query scale around 2^85.3, which does not directly translate to a payment forgery. Inspect fresh random masking rows for regular and product polynomials, openings at zeta and g·zeta, per-leaf salt on wires/products/quotients and effective masking in proof serialization. The public constants/sigmas use a preprocessed oracle. Public fees, anchor heights, nullifiers, output count, time and network origin persist across any replacement of the proof layer. ## Registered direct proof and wire audit

The 1481-byte verifier SHA-256 is `5d6cfc6ccff5792e4899799124b2fb1e7dac50f727825c4886485dc01a564ce8`. A separate parser reads its verifier cap, declared and effective FRI settings, masking flags and actual dimensions. The padded trace has 4,096 rows, the LDE 131,072 evaluation points, the reduction five folds by four, and the final polynomial four quadratic-extension coefficients. Two permutation challenges, seven partial products, four common constants, a quotient factor of eight, and 26 public words determine the emitted proof size. Opening accounting yields 1,016 regular mask rows and 1,018 pairs of product rows, or 3,052 rows altogether. The driver's 455 gates before finalization count different work. The Rust circuit builder executes random value generators and uses the fork's `rand` feature, and its oracle builder adds four independently sampled field words per leaf of each witness-dependent oracle. Random-looking bytes alone cannot establish the sampling distribution or a simulator. [Exact circuit builder](https://docs.rs/crate/qp-plonky2/1.5.5/source/src/plonk/circuit_builder.rs) · [Oracle salts](https://docs.rs/crate/qp-plonky2/1.5.5/source/src/fri/oracle.rs) · [Profile and target checks](../direct/profile.rs)

`direct_wire.py` derives 139,594 proof bytes and 143,187 packet bytes from those dimensions and fixed receiving lengths. It consumes the actual caps, openings, 26 query rounds with nine Merkle paths each, final polynomial, PoW witness and trailing length-prefixed public statement. The first public packet at H2, SHA-256 `1f531ad357648edb97a3ec824af8549314d7094962bf48b6fcc43d421fd80f50`, had all 78 explicit salt tuples nonzero and distinct. The separate `receipt_check.rs` linked directly to the locked `qp-poseidon-core` 3.1.0 primitive and recomputed the digest in public inputs 22–25 over `qnero/ct`, the count, both lengths and both complete ciphertexts. It matched for the canonical order and differed with reversed receipts. Fee was 3. These checks describe proof shape and ciphertext binding. Public cryptographic validity is checked separately by the real verifier.

Running `python3 -IB experiments/payment-architecture/review/budget.py` reconstructs the legacy measured 15-block selected archive from raw placement, PoW IDs and packet lengths. The registered sparse/busy averages give 6,036/6,332 B of singleton proof headroom at 1 TB and 46,036/46,332 B at 5 TB. `BUDGET.json` retains the exact counts and several boundary cases. A fixed 2-second opportunity clock with at most four inclusions per block carries at most 63,072,000 inclusions per 365 days. The 100M scenarios instead imply mean intervals of 0.1907928 s (sparse) and 0.993384 s (busy), so the eight-block window spans 1.5263424 s or 7.947072 s at mean spacing. Actual PoW intervals vary. A proof slow enough to age out in a chosen scenario creates retry traffic despite available aggregate CPU or archival space.

The opening vectors alone explain a limit of ordinary path deduplication. Each queried initial row carries 64 preprocessed evaluations, 135 wires, 16 product/auxiliary evaluations, 16 quotient evaluations and 12 salt words. At 8 B per word and 26 distinct rows in the measured packet, this totals **50,544 B** before authentication paths, the remaining proof and the receipts. Compact Merkle multiproofs that retain those initial rows cannot meet the 5 TB scenario. Regenerating deterministic preprocessed evaluations from the public circuit at the verifier would move computation to each node. Altering the number/width of evaluations or using a different commitment/opening proof changes the bound.
