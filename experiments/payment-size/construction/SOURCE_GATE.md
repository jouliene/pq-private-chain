# Complete proof construction gate

Public reference `8f9bae9b96248e6c147acf9664fd34bb727cb756`. CP03 fixes the framed Goldilocks payment relation, EF5 challenges, SHA3-512 roles, B16/q160 direct FRI, and zero grinding. Its own composition qualifications remain open. A numerical whole-protocol PQ level for that profile has never been established. All sizes below identify their relation and provenance.

## Direct hiding WHIR

**The retained adapter cannot prove the payment relation by itself.** Its input is `Poly<F>` and its opening protocol is `Vec<Point<EF>>`. It reveals the requested evaluations. The November 16 revision of Chiesa, Fenzi and Weissenberg gives the R1CS reduction in **Theorem 12.2 and Construction 12.3**. The prover encodes its witness together with three independent terminal masks and sumcheck-mask coordinates. Each terminal mask is a random multiple of `X²−X`. The final sumcheck challenge excludes 0 and 1. The three final evaluation claims use those masks. A batched linear form over the *same* encoded message closes the relation. This is a different opening interface from revealing an MLE at an arbitrary application point. The published theorem concerns a specified interactive construction under its code, sampling and distance hypotheses. [Paper](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/391) and [retained adapter](../../../source/experiments/compact-challenge-v1/view01/whir/src/pcs/zk/adapter.rs).

**Ethereum Spartan-WHIR is a concrete positive source lead for an outer masking layer.** Revision `14d6da4211eda59e65cfd0af5e2dcd89d523edc3` pins Client-Side-Proving/Plonky3 `458ef546a251ab4d9d73c453d24a414334786ca0`. The public 2-round arithmetic example gives a full-rank three-by-three mask minor in our independent replay. For a non-Boolean challenge `x`, the coefficient of the independent quadratic parameter is `x²(1−x)`, so one fresh independent parameter per matrix already spans the three terminal outputs. That calculation covers only these three coordinates at a fixed challenge. It omits the earlier correlated transcript and remaining PCS observations. Code uses a different mask layout and separate group closure from Construction 12.3. Establish joint simulator and extractor correspondence before claiming the theorem applies. [Prover and verifier](https://github.com/ethereum/spartan-whir/blob/14d6da4211eda59e65cfd0af5e2dcd89d523edc3/src/protocol.rs) and [masked sumcheck](https://github.com/ethereum/spartan-whir/blob/14d6da4211eda59e65cfd0af5e2dcd89d523edc3/src/sumcheck.rs).

The stock backend is KoalaBear. Eight digest elements offer at most `4 log2(p) = 123.954739` ideal classical birthday bits. The implementation caps the requested component target at 123; a five-fold, 34-commitment example reserves seven bits of Merkle slack and permits at most 116 composed target bits. Other fold and argument counts have their own caps. The public, four-constraint, 80-bit DirectSparse fixture has 52,884 bytes of *guest input*. Select a zero-knowledge linear-code encoding with space for *joint* masks and a hiding opening of the theorem's actual linear form. Repeating the defective example or changing `security_level` alone would supply none of this evidence. ## A transparent recursive wrapper

An independent route runs the *existing* CP03 verifier inside a fixed program using only the canonical public statement and a candidate CP03 proof. Pin the arity to 2 and every public frame/program/profile/parameter. The public outer verifier must compare the actual canonical statement to its expected input, verify the fixed program identity, authenticate any preprocessing, require successful halt and consume a fully resolved transparent receipt. The ordinary ledger must still authenticate receipt/context/supply inputs as it does for CP03. A verifier program selected by the proof producer or a journal reporting a host verdict would prove a different statement. [CP03 verification boundary](../../../source/experiments/compact-all-payments-v3/src/engine.rs).

A randomized function of the public-safe CP03 proof and statement inherits their privacy under post-processing, provided it receives no additional confidential input or auxiliary state. This gives a reason to consider even a nonhiding *outer* argument for this specific role. Soundness is more demanding. Accepting the existence of some computationally valid inner proof requires a compatible outer extraction or composition argument tying a successful outer prover to such a proof, followed by the inner CP03 authorization assumptions. Each hash, field, recursion level, challenge schedule and adaptive/QROM loss needs a soundness account. A signature, elliptic-curve Groth16 receipt or the outer VM's development mode cannot supply the required research role. [Transparent recursive receipts](https://dev.risczero.com/api/3.0/recursion).

**Measured input and derived cost floor.** On the checked-in `payer2x2.body`, the main opening contains 159 distinct salted rows and 918 frontier digests. Reconstructing their single binary root takes at least 1,076 binary compressions. The framed 584-word main row plus eight salt words costs 67 SHA3-512 sponge permutations; each internal node costs three. A wrapper executing this exact check must account for at least **13,881 Keccak-f[1600] permutations in that tree alone**. The complete guest also verifies other trees, FRI algebra, transcript, codec and frame. This is an operation-count bound, not guest cycles, memory or time. The vendor describes a transparent RISC Zero 3.0 succinct receipt as about 200 kB, already above the research 175,126-byte complete-body screen if that estimate carries over. It recommends at least 16 GB for local proving. The next justified RISC Zero action would check specified strength and run a public CP03 guest verifier with the exact SHA3-512 path in a metered executor before authorizing any proof job. No guest, proving run or immutable RISC Zero profile was admitted. [Recursion](https://dev.risczero.com/api/3.0/recursion) and [local memory context](https://dev.risczero.com/api/3.0/zkvm).

For a WHIR-based VM, the inspected leanVM project explicitly lists zero-knowledge support as planned and advertises 128-bit ROM/64-bit QROM soundness. As above, an outer wrapper might tolerate a nonhiding VM, but an unmodified direct wallet-witness guest would require hiding. Its quoted soundness also needs an explicit non-weakening comparison. [Upstream description](https://github.com/leanEthereum/leanVM).

## Decision and reproduction

Start an actual complete benchmark with the reversible CP03 representation already being evaluated in the candidate lane. Equality of reconstructed proof bytes allows the same verifier to check the same payment under its existing qualifications. The direct WHIR and recursive designs above are separately scoped challengers. Their results justify neither an unconditional proof-size forecast nor a production or PQ assurance claim.

The following commands check public Git blobs and replay arithmetic on published unit-vector constants and one local public CP03 body. They do not compile upstream dependencies or generate a payment. Run from the public project root, using fresh temporary directories or prior checkouts containing the two named revisions.

```sh
tmpdir=$(mktemp -d)
git clone --filter=blob:none --no-checkout https://github.com/ethereum/spartan-whir.git "$tmpdir/spartan"
git clone --filter=blob:none --no-checkout https://github.com/worldfnd/provekit.git "$tmpdir/provekit"
python3 -I experiments/payment-size/construction/check_geometry.py "$tmpdir/spartan" "$tmpdir/provekit" > "$tmpdir/geometry.json"
diff -u experiments/payment-size/construction/geometry.json "$tmpdir/geometry.json"
```

## Independent ceiling and acceptance map for the CP03 representation

I inspected the candidate `transport.rs`, `transcript.rs` and `main.rs` at Git blob IDs `d3d9e635b1c5159cd8aa4163b905b13434a86af1`, `fe811369d21964e02f4fa04ef98b88abbcbb9247` and `a8ce6855e4bd5f10bf314c6c1681272618d1d8d2`. The packing code compares every omitted fixed opening against a fresh public opening and checks equality at repeated coordinates. It uses child coordinates for FRI siblings and parent coordinates for salts. The expansion code computes coordinates from the original typed transcript schedule, rebuilds the deterministic fixed tree from approved public inputs, checks its root against the original registry, restores the full envelope, and calls the original verifier on the canonical statement. `run` also requires the two-input statement, the applicable fee floor and binding of both actual ciphertexts. No private state is required for this transformation. Ledger transitions and historical availability remain separate.

Let `Expand(x,c)` either reject or output `p`, where `x` includes the application statement and public verifier-owned parameters. The candidate acceptance predicate includes `VerifyCP03(x,p)`. Thus a successful candidate prover yields a successful CP03 prover for the same statement by public, efficient expansion, with no additional cryptographic argument or extraction layer. Any CP03 knowledge guarantee applicable to this composed prover has the same unresolved model restrictions as the original. For honest proofs `c = Pack(x,p)`, observable lengths and duplicate-query patterns are publicly computable from `x,p`. Efficient post-processing preserves the corresponding CP03 indistinguishability property under the same assumptions, including independent honest randomness. This reasoning requires an honest producer and covers the ledger transcript; it imposes no constraint on deliberate auxiliary communications by a malicious payer. Canonical ledger identity, choice of body hash and any coexistence of both encodings need a later protocol decision.

`compaction_floor.py` independently scans wire sections without reconstructing the prover's transcript. Removing by repeated coordinate cannot omit more rows than removing *every* repeated byte string within each row/salt section. All nonfixed frontier digests stay in this candidate. This yields a rigorous ceiling on the potential savings for each measured body, independent of any assumption about random-field compressibility.

Both bodies retain at least **742,848 distinct main-row value bytes alone**, over four times the complete-body screen. They also retain 335,488 and 331,776 bytes of nonfixed frontier digests respectively. The bound covers this chosen grammar. It neither proves an information-theoretic lower bound on arbitrary encodings nor bounds other proof systems. Semantically deriving both fixed OOD evaluations from authenticated public preprocessing could additionally address up to `2 × 114 × 40 = 9,120` wire bytes before any costs, but cannot close this gap. A different opening protocol or trace layout is required for the next order-of-magnitude attempt.

An approximately 15% saving scales to roughly 27 decimal TB per 100M analogous bodies per first stored copy. That could be an operational improvement while remaining far from the predeclared tenfold screen. Actual fresh-payment percentages and resource costs belong to the measured campaign. Of the assessed options the reversible CP03 representation has the strongest implementation and security correspondence for a **bounded complete comparison**. A valid complete proof under this representation establishes a smaller encoding of CP03; it does not establish a materially different compact proving system. Defer consensus selection until the complete-body result, verification costs, operational benefit and alternate constructions are weighed together.
