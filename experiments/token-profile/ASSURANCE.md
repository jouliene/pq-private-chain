# Spend-proof security budget

## Decision threshold and adversary

The prototype gate is a reproducible experiment with disposable assets. An independent node must derive its verifier from fixed source, authenticate the founding inventory, verify a complete spend from public data, and enforce each asset's integer equation, accepted anchor, nullifier uniqueness and atomic admission. An offline recipient must reconstruct the same commitment and spend it from seed. A successful run licenses research with this relation. For a prospective value-bearing deployment we propose a separate target of at least 128 bits of classical work-equivalent and 128 bits of quantum work-equivalent against **unauthorized value creation or spending**. This is a research design target. There is no established end-to-end figure for these profiles. Defining resources also requires a quantum gate/depth/memory budget, oracle-query budget, number of targets, lifetime and success probability.

Allow adaptive spenders, miners and certificate builders to collude, choose statements and timing, and reuse public data. A local validator chooses the chain, approved verifier, fee policy and state independently. Secret theft and consensus capture are separate failures. For privacy, compare honest wallet executions with the same allowed public leakage under a passive public observer, a relaying node that sees retries, a counterparty with a view key, and a later seed compromise. The stronger adversary can record traffic today and run quantum computation later. Quantum query bounds below refer to idealized oracles, not measured gate costs on a physical computer.

## Registered statements and transcript

The reproducible decoder walks both binary verifiers through the gate list and end-of-file, and walks real proofs in the shipped archive. The key does not encode names for public-input positions, the extension type or the choice of transcript hash. Those come from the registered verifier program and its pinned types. The decoder records the actual 26-word direct statement and all eight 26-word statements in the observed certificate. Native verification and independent state replay are separate steps. Both have 135 wires, 60 routed, cap height 4, maximum and actual quotient factor 8, two PLONK repetitions, zero lookup polynomials, configured security 128, Goldilocks prime p=2^64−2^32+1, and extension degree 2. The public verifier checks the whole ordered receipt digest against the available receipts. An attacker who pays herself according to her own openings can choose which receipt bytes to publish. Correct encryption of the opening to the intended payee is an honest-sender/receiver check. The cryptographic proof alone also accepts no chain selection decision. The quotient cap precedes the quadratic point zeta. Claimed openings precede the quadratic FRI batching coin. Each reduction cap precedes its quadratic folding coin. ## Numerical bounds and their domains

* **Exact integer bounds.** With M=2^62−1, two inputs contribute at most 2M. Two outputs plus the consensus fee of three contribute at most 2M+3 < p. Under per-coordinate range checks, a field equality for that coordinate implies integer equality. DLF1 has only one such equation. A typed coin requires the second coordinate in each committed and recovered opening, an independent token equation, an authenticated origin, and a unique spend identity for the entire vector. [Monetary bounds and obligations](money/REVIEW.md)
* **Interactive coins.** A fixed nonzero polynomial of degree d independent of a uniform challenge has at most d roots; a single evaluation over F or F² therefore costs up to d/p or d/p² in error. For illustration a degree-4096 polynomial over F² yields about 116 bits of single-test error exponent and degree 65536 about 112. Those are examples for a fixed polynomial. Quotient composition, shared points, permutation products, batching, exceptional denominators, transcript adaptivity and every round need their actual degrees and a reduction. Two sets of base-field coins are not automatically two independently failed payment checks.
* **FRI.** The constructor uses min(bit_length(p²), 26×5+0)=128. The second term 130 is a heuristic. For a *fixed* set of t accepted indices of N and independent uniform field draws, the heaviest probability per query under the actual modulo map is (floor(p/N)×t+min(t,p mod N))/p. The exponent from 26 draws is near 130, 52 or 26 when t/N is 1/32, 1/4 or 1/2. For both real domains p mod N=1. These are reproducible conditional query terms. They assume the specified agreement and do not establish it for an adaptive adversary. Noninteractive FRI needs commit-phase errors, folding and correlated agreement/list bounds, followed by the losses of compilation. Published results for parameter sets with different rates, fields, domain sizes or round schedules cannot provide this key's security figure. [Constructor](https://docs.rs/crate/qp-plonky2/1.5.5/source/src/plonk/circuit_builder.rs) · [FRI and Plonk-like reductions](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/1071) · [Concrete noninteractive analysis](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/1161)
* **Hash assumptions.** Four canonical Goldilocks limbs have p⁴ possible digests. An ideal random function on this output space has generic arbitrary-collision query scale around 2^128 classically and 2^85.3 quantumly, and fixed-target preimage scales around 2^256 and 2^128 respectively. At n independent marked targets the ideal fixed-preimage scales become p⁴/n and sqrt(p⁴/n). The query model omits memory/depth. The actual original Poseidon used by the argument has width 12, rate 8, capacity 4, x^7, eight full and 22 partial rounds. The upstream maintainers cite an algebraic estimate around 95 bits for that profile. This estimate does not establish an attack on a payment, or any concrete lower bound on every use. Review the required property separately for Merkle binding, fixed-length transcript absorption, note commitments, nullifier pseudorandomness and the additional typed opening. The new opening uses Poseidon2. Widening only the block ID would leave both proof levels unchanged. [Upstream security analysis](https://github.com/0xPolygonZero/plonky2#security) · [BBLP analysis](https://tosc.iacr.org/index.php/ToSC/article/view/9850) · [Quantum collision query model](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9705002)
* **Noninteraction and quantum access.** In a fixed-marked-fraction search illustration with error ε, q independent classical trials have success at most qε. Quantum amplitude amplification can reach constant success at order 1/sqrt(ε) ideal coherent queries. This illustration cannot convert 130 to 65 bits for the complete protocol. A quantum Fiat–Shamir argument must match the multi-round sponge transcript, commit/open compilation and oracle domains. Generic measure-and-reprogram losses grow with the query budget and the number of challenge stages. An IOP compiler theorem has premises concerning round-by-round soundness and its oracle. Neither supplies the concrete multi-theorem extractor for these pinned binaries or instantiates an algebraic hash as a quantum random oracle. [Multi-round Fiat–Shamir](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.05207) · [Succinct arguments in the QROM](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/834) · [Amplitude amplification](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0005055)
* **Ownership, recursion and lifetime.** A valid witness must open each real member under its spend key. Computational knowledge of that witness by a malicious prover, binding of the selected member and uniqueness of the real nullifier are distinct assumptions. For a malicious certificate, a reduction needs accepting inner proofs from the outer prover. Given an extraction reduction with failure ε_ext, outer error ε_out and at most eight applicable inner error bounds ε_in, a union accounting can contain ε_ext+ε_out+8ε_in, with each argument evaluated at the induced time and query budgets. Reuse of one oracle across levels, adaptive statements, padding and repeated history require justification for applying those bounds. This expression gives no numerical bit guarantee. Likewise a per-attempt bound ε conditional on every prior public history would allow an Nε cumulative bound. At N=100M, log2 N≈26.58, but yearly honest payment count supplies no upper bound on adversarial trials. ## External review required before assets of value

1. Establish concrete classical and quantum knowledge bounds for the registered arities, gates, quotient/DEEP checks, base-field permutation coins, exact FRI reductions and multi-proof adaptive Fiat–Shamir. Include adversary budgets, all loss terms, and an independently reviewed choice of commitment and transcript hash.
2. Audit note commitment binding, spend authority, pseudorandom and distinct real/dummy nullifiers, cross-domain encodings, leaf selection and native/recursive equivalence. Review the typed preimage, both ranges, both equations, nullifier scope, fee handling, authenticated issuance, unique inventory and migrations in the precise activated chain.
3. Establish whole-transcript zero knowledge under adaptive multi-user and chosen-statement observation. Include related proofs for common inputs, both receipts, ciphertext recovery, retained archive, network/retry timing, wallet randomness and seed compromise. Review recipient anonymity separately from standardized ML-KEM-1024 confidentiality and AEAD integrity. Quantify per-user/multi-target advantage under the actual sender/receiver algorithms and define the permitted leakage. [Privacy measurements and games](privacy/PRIVACY.md) · [FIPS 203](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/203/final)
4. For any new archival version, fix its permitted inner verifier and state/effect identity. Prove the extraction/composition statement and check it on an empty validator after deleting original proofs. Existing D8C1 is a DLF1-only authorization source. Continued availability across independent archivists remains a backlog experiment.
