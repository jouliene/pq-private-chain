# SWP1 independent review registration

Base revision `f65a8b3c51fdc8aebdc2138552c45e81f91f5e70`. The research fixture exchanges seven existing fixed units for thirty existing managed units. Each proposed leg consumes its author's private notes and creates two standard spendable note commitments. One party pays the agreed three native units. The reviewer owns no node, wallet, circuit or demonstration code.

## Authorization and extraction

An unconditional old-format payment proof can be stripped out of a wrapper. Ordinary MNG1 requires three native units per leg. Reusing two ordinary proofs therefore exposes unilateral settlement and charges six. A virtual-header construction using the funded root of a selected ancestor could bind a proposal to its partner while retaining the old verifier. Its two fixed MNG1 fees still conflict with the requested one-fee exchange. A single jointly proved circuit could express the exchange, but combining both witnesses in an ordinary prover would expose spending data to the prover. A private joint proving protocol would require its own implementation and measurement.

The selected SLG1 proof must independently bind note ownership, membership, nullifiers, two output commitments, the ordered receipt digest, the leg's native fee, zero aggregate supply change, and all 256 bits of its one-time authorization key. Both SLH-DSA signatures must authorize the identical network, expiry, agreed payer, blinded terms commitment and ordered complete proposed leg bytes. This avoids a dependency between generating a leg proof and obtaining its counterparty's signature. A party can withhold its signature. A party with both valid signatures can publish the full exchange until it expires or becomes ineligible.

Before releasing any final authorization, each wallet must reconcile its stored offer, its own actual proposed leg, and a locally verified opening of the other party's signed output. A receipt's decryptability alone does not establish ownership of the committed note. The wallet reconstructs the commitment using its own spending public key, the decrypted native amount, asset and quantity, and the actual note opening. Its expected incoming payment and exact fee responsibility must match its local instructions. The recipient can exercise its spend key after seed restoration with the ordinary payment circuit.

## Independent checks

- Run the standard entry point from a detached anonymous HTTPS checkout. Use fresh private run directories and independent seeds. Check registered verifier keys and exact operator-approved executable hashes. Record compilation and live-run measurements separately.
- Decode complete proof public inputs, ciphertext digests, and both signatures from the public wire. Recompute the signature message outside the implementation and verify positive and altered messages using a separate SLH-DSA provider. Exercise isolated registered public proof verification, including alternate historical interpretations of a leg. Never provide private terms, wallet seeds or spend witnesses to this process.
- Reconstruct every published PoW block and its two physical swap seats, fee/reward totals, funded commitments, spent sets and branch-specific asset supplies. Check that neither an incomplete pool offer nor a half-frame block can advance monetary state. Check duplicates and ordinary input conflicts in both directions, raw proof and receipt mutations, reordered/foreign partners, and the expiry boundary at candidate inclusion height. Keep an intact positive control beside malformed-input controls.
- Compare both post-fork states, fresh-node replay and seed-restored inventories. Confirm each independent recipient's subsequent ordinary payment actually spends its incoming exchange note. Join commitments and nullifiers to participant-local databases only in the disposable run and publish aggregates. Include actual fees, native and token change, total supply per asset and miner credits.
- Measure complete joint network bytes, each physical block frame, added block framing, each registered proof, both signatures and receipt ciphertexts. Keep per-role wall time, CPU and peak memory separate. Identify concurrent operation and measurement boundaries. Two legs consume two of four existing physical slots; historical frame and block caps still apply.
- Record what a chain observer learns from public messages (an exchange, two ordered legs, expiry, payer seat, equal-time inclusion, public outputs, the fixed counterparty and change output positions, nullifiers, ciphertexts and fresh authorization keys). Participant-disclosed economic amounts are testing evidence. Keep private negotiation salts, addresses, keys, opening data, outboxes, databases and protocol negotiation files outside the public capture.

The functional run and finite rejection cases support the reported test environment. Security of the proof, hybrid receipt construction, private opening, hiding commitment, pseudorandomness of per-session keys and a quantum adversary are separate assumptions.
