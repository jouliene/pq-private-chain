# SmallWood+ assurance for hidden funded membership

The November 2026 paper supplies a concrete interactive construction and a useful size discriminator. Its Keccak benchmark has different input, output and wiring constraints from the funded paths. The paper's extractor reads classical prover hash queries. A 128-bit quantum authorization claim for the FS01 payment remains open.

An FS01 parent has 131 input bytes. At a 1,088-bit SHAKE256 rate the state contains 24 tag/level bits, two 512-bit children, 40 padding bits and 512 zero capacity bits. Thus 576 input bits are fixed and every bit of the 512-bit squeeze feeds its consumer. For a two-parent test the first 512-bit digest feeds the next ordered pair privately, while the public verifier checks the entire final digest. In the two-path payment, 78 parent-to-parent edges carry 39,936 private bit copies, two leaf-to-parent edges add 1,024, and all 80 sibling orders depend on hidden path bits. A binary conditional swap can share one product per bit across both output children, giving 40,960 binary products for this elementary ordering circuit. Copies may be native wire reuse, equality constraints, or additional sum-checks depending on representation. These counts do not translate to a per-copy byte tariff. There are 99 permutations across the full FS01 leg, including other domains and multi-block hashes. A public CICO endpoint in place of an internal digest could identify the associated public subtree to ledger observers. ## What the interactive proof provides

HVZK and bad-oracle probabilities inherit the LCS bounds. | Make the public statement, private selector bitness, same digest/ASK across all roles and terminal projections explicit in the actual circuit. The sampled last rank-doubling step for GF(2) to GF(2^128) rejected 71,196 of 100,000 draws over 20 sampled prefixes. At that observed rate this one step requires roughly 3.47 draws on average. This measurement concerns honest work. The paper derives the retried coordinate using a counter and checks the final hiding predicate. A concrete Fiat–Shamir proof needs an injective counter encoding, domain and context binding, and an analysis covering all counter trials. The experiment does not bound an adaptive quantum simulator. ### Local linked circuit and stronger fields

The project implementation takes another route through Compiler 1. It builds the two fixed parent permutations as sparse R1CS, uses the four interpolation points of a cubic sum-check, checks a single terminal product by §3.2 linearization, and batches seven resulting linear checks into one LCS. The immutable reconstruction from the public level and 512-bit output pins the circuit. In this diagnostic both the base and challenge field are GF(2^16). Independent inspection found 17 binary index variables for the row-equality polynomial (the padded matrix has 2^17 rows), 81,412 witness field entries, 81,922 constraints, and 56 masked or linearization prover elements. Of 87,760 payload bytes, 82,048 are opened field columns, 4,096 are sibling digests, 256 are leaf tapes, 1,024 are completion evaluations, 160 are Q, 64 are two digests and 112 are the masked or linearization field elements. This breakdown reconstructs the observed size from the serializer. The verifier reads the complete Q and separate authentication path for each opening. [Circuit](../smallwood/relation.py) · [Transcript and checks](../smallwood/sumcheck.py) · [LCS and DECS](../smallwood/commitment.py)

For the local N=256, s=32, s'=16, dP=47, dQ=79, and degree-enforcing dimension 16 the corresponding negative log2 error bounds are 79.655 for the degree test, 28.800 for the subset test, 16 for LCS linear mixing, and 11.913 for the row equality. All apply to specific interactive classical steps. The bit-string domains, proof/statement hash, canonical wire encoding and fixed-circuit commitment are project choices. Neither the field width nor these finite checks imply 128-bit linked-proof security.

The polynomial mask in the corrected implementation has the §4.2 Remark 7 form M = L + X^32 H, with deg L, deg H ≤ 47. The code samples a uniform 16-coefficient overlap while preserving M. At 16 distinct opening points, the overlap evaluation map is a bijective Vandermonde map, so the extra exposed chunk can be simulated from fresh uniform values even conditioned on M. The remaining chunk follows from M and the first one. This detail matters because the serializer opens both chunks, whereas the proof's hiding hybrid discusses the combined M(E). The degree bound of 79 also holds for every pair of extracted degree-47 chunks. The degree argument and local hiding hybrid do not resolve Fiat–Shamir or payment composition. [Implementation](../smallwood/commitment.py) · [Preprint, Remark 7 and Appendix D.5, proof of Thm. 4](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2880)

At the second executed profile the same circuit uses F=K=GF(2^256), 1,024 leaves, 32 columns, 84 openings and four degree-enforcing combinations. The 256-bit salts and 512-bit hash outputs are distinct parameters. Independent arithmetic verifies that the degree-256 polynomial with non-leading terms X^10 + X^5 + X^2 + 1 is irreducible over GF(2). The Frobenius power X^(2^256) reduces to X, and the gcd of X^(2^128)+X with the polynomial is 1, covering the sole prime divisor of 256. The two-chunk argument above now uses 84 independent overlap coefficients, with degree bounds dP=115 and dQ=147. The measured packet has 6,926,976 bytes. Of these, 6,859,776 are opened columns, 53,760 are siblings, 2,688 are salts, 4,096 are completion values, 4,736 are Q, 128 are two digests and 1,792 are trace elements. The exact negative log2 error bounds for the classical degree, subset, mixing and row checks are 503.745, 273.742, 256 and 251.913. Replacing κ in the *hypothetical* CMS screen by the row bound gives t²κ = 17 at t = 2^128. The bound has already ceased to give constant-success control before compiler, composition and implementation costs. This establishes a limitation of this correspondence and bound; an attack at that work factor has not been demonstrated. ### A full-relation lower bound for the literal binary R1CS port

A specific representation can be screened without selecting a row width or tree size. Translate the published FS01 binary circuit literally, allocating one GF(2^16) witness element for every AND output and private input and folding affine operations into public linear maps. This gives W = 3,854,992 + 49,233 = 3,904,225 witness cells before constants or trace masks. Use the uncompressed generic R1CS witness of Compiler 1. Binary ring packing, a different circuit, Compiler 2 and structured Keccak GKR each change this assumption. [FS01 binary geometry](../../fresh-spend/proof/SIZES.json) · [SmallWood+, §4.3](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2880)

Let s be the row width, t the number of opened positions, b the number of bits in K, and e the requested error exponent for each of the two local tests. For a nonvacuous opening check N > 2s + t − 1. The hypergeometric error in Thm. 4 is at least (2s/N)^t, since its successive factors are (2s+j)/(N−j). The DECS evaluation domain lies in GF(2^16), so even allowing the entire field gives the necessary inequality (2s/65,536)^t ≤ 2^-e. Thm. 1's stated DECS test certificate requires at least ceil(e/16) degree-enforcing combinations. In the §4.3 serializer, three disjoint sets of values consequently require at least the following number of bits.

```text
2 s b + 16 t ceil(W/s) + 16 ceil(e/16) (s+t)
```

These terms respectively pay for the Q evaluations even with Remark 8 compression, opened witness rows, and degree masks/completion values. The script minimizes the relaxed expression over every positive integer s < 32,768 with the smallest qualifying t certified by exact integer inequalities. It omits trace transmissions, DECS roots, paths, tapes, check coefficients, outer messages and all packet bytes. The minimizers might not constitute feasible complete profiles, which only makes the floor more optimistic. The screening condition here is to meet the two local errors **through the paper's bounds**; the analysis does not establish an optimal error or a lower bound for other proof systems.

| b / e | Payload floor in bytes | Relaxed minimizing s / t |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 128 / 128 | 219,684 | 1,872 / 31 |
| 256 / 256 | 440,222 | 1,786 / 61 |
| 512 / 288 | 584,992 | 1,448 / 64 |
| 512 / 416 | 758,744 | 1,676 / 97 |

Thus this literal Compiler 1 port at the three higher local profiles already exceeds the provisional 256 KiB screen on proof data alone, under every packing allowed by this calculation. Preserving Keccak structure or using the specialized packing can change that outcome. Increasing the challenge extension while committing every binary gate to its own base-field cell is unlikely to recover a compact full FS01 proof.

## Quantum extraction and lifetime

Definition 9 gives the efficient extractor the ordered queries **and answers** of the prover, together with the transcript. Appendix D.1 recovers committed polynomial entries using this classical information. The bad-transcript bound for one DECS is `3q(q+1)/2^(2λ+1)`; the HVZK term is `q/2^λ`. For q = 2^128 and λ = 128 the two bounds are already vacuous even in that classical counting exercise. At λ = 256 the single-instance classical hiding term becomes 2^-128. These are upper-bound evaluations, not feasible forgery probabilities or quantum bounds. The public-coin argument's RBR result includes adaptively chosen statements in its stated ROM experiment. A quantum superposition query has no classical chronological query/answer list of this form. [Preprint, Definitions 7–9, Thms. 1–5 and Appendix D.1](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2880)

A route worth investigating is to formulate the entire masked interactive protocol as an information-theoretic IOP and use a source-matched BCS commitment and Fiat–Shamir transform. CMS Theorem 8.6 takes an IOP with the knowledge-prefix property of Definition 8.5 and gives extraction probability asymptotically bounded in terms of the adversary's success, `t²κ` and `t³/2^h`. The query allowance includes a verifier query surcharge. The theorem also has a ZK statement for an HVZK IOP. Applying it requires a mapping for the actual DECS/opening transcript, rank-conditioned challenges and terminal observations. The SmallWood+ interactive CP theorem does not supply that mapping. Alternatively a compressed-oracle extraction proof may address DECS directly. Existing tight three-move commit-and-open results do not immediately cover interleaved challenge-dependent polynomial and evaluation messages. [CMS, Theorem 8.6](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/834.pdf) · [Don, Fehr, Majenz and Schaffner](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.13730)

For sensitivity only, substitute t = 2^128 with unit constants. For each term to be below one separately would need κ < 2^-256 and h > 384. For each to be at most 2^-128 separately would need κ ≤ 2^-384 and h ≥ 512. Reserving an overall probability for their sum, constants and other reductions costs additional margin. If a theorem supplied a uniform conditional per-proof bound and one chose to allot the full t separately per event, union-bounding over 2.2 billion proof-bearing legs adds 31.035 bits. At 4.4 billion receipt slots the analogous count costs 32.035 bits. These counts assume 100M included operations annually for 20 years, with 90% ordinary and 10% two-leg swaps. A global-query theorem may already cover adaptive attempts and then multiplying again would double count. Offered traffic alone puts no upper bound on attempted forgeries or chosen verification/scan queries. No one of these calculations is an application assurance bound. ## Ledger and receipt privacy

The two-parent proof can hide its child order and intermediate digests only within a specified transcript game. A 64-row global lookup permits privately choosing among the validator-authenticated occupied rows at its anchor. Occupied count and public prior funding metadata still refine the anonymity set. Replacing a global set with a publicly identified shard changes the game. Both procedures must be tested against matched public histories, identical statement shapes, local wallet-only witness generation, no identifying fetches, and fresh independent proof randomness across retries. A public-only process demonstrates the verifier's data boundary. It cannot establish indistinguishability of the resulting transcripts.

A whole-payment game needs the joint history of nullifiers, note commitments, both receipt ciphertexts, intent/authorization, proof bytes, fees, input-role policy, anchors, swap approvals and later spends, with adaptive choices and repeated owners. First simulate the proof given the common public statement and correlated receipt side information under the correct oracle model. Then supply the exact receiver-key anonymity and ciphertext assumptions for both real envelopes in that history, including honest descriptor selection in the challenge, adversarial funding outside it, and the prescribed wallet failure behavior. An authorized payer knows whom it paid. Reusing inputs intentionally repeats real NFs. A later backup disclosure opens old ciphertexts. Source-level hiding of a masked MLE alone does not resolve any of these boundaries. [FS01 receipt and retry games](../../fresh-spend/binding/GAMES.md)

The result justifies a wired private research experiment, with the exact backend and parameter strength recorded. Ranking it for funded admission requires a prover and independent public verifier on the full same-witness statement, measured costs and a credible quantum extraction/joint simulation route. The published CICO value cannot set that rank.
