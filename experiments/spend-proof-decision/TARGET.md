# Spend-proof decision target

Starting revision `3f08bff0089096d7fb27279bc0d444006e9147ce`. Register before candidate comparison. The research design target is at least 128 bits of **post-quantum work** for a constant-success violation of spend knowledge, monetary integrity, or computational witness/recipient privacy in the stated game. This is a design target, not an existing certified property or the meaning of a library security flag. Also report classical estimates. Give the event, number of targets, allowed queries and concrete loss with any bound. Do not equate 128-bit single-instance statistical soundness, ideal collision cost, Grover query cost, bit security of a primitive, and real adversarial gate cost.

The adversary may control both network delivery and other wallets, submit adaptive chosen public statements and proofs, observe multiple forks and retries, keep transcripts and receipts indefinitely, and run quantum algorithms including superposition access to public hash computations in the QROM analysis. Protocol input/output messages to honest wallets and ledger verification are classical. Model private witnesses, ephemeral randomness and backups as unavailable unless a corruption game explicitly grants access. Consider classical malicious provers as well. Distinguish compromise after recording, malicious sender receipt failure, disclosure by a swap counterparty, passive ledger observation and network metadata. Correctly validating nodes enforce canonical parsing, selected-branch membership, resource bounds, current nullifiers, unique authorized issuance and atomic updates. PoW finality, hardware compromise, real-world identity and fair delivery by a withholding last signer are separate.

Compare an ordinary funded two-input/two-output spend and two independently generated swap legs, zero supply delta for transfers, native conservation/fees, fungible private asset ID and amounts, real output receipts, backup recovery and both final swap authorizations. Existing funded openings matter. Any changed relation, ciphertext, witness, commitment, transfer/dummy fee, asset identification, ephemeral key, or verification assumption must be reported. Distinguish same economic semantics from byte-compatible histories. A stronger proof that still authenticates an old commitment inherits its binding/ownership risk. Passing correct executions cannot establish knowledge, hiding or a quantum reduction.
