# Funded spend proof from circuit to security claim

Revision `3f08bff0089096d7fb27279bc0d444006e9147ce`. Both registered verifiers check a genuine arithmetic argument. The fund-ownership relation and its integer bounds are identifiable in source. The available reductions do not establish the registered target of 128 bits of post-quantum work for knowledge, monetary integrity or witness privacy. The independent baseline ordinary spend and its two swap legs reproduced 78 of 78 query positions against the registered Merkle caps. The matched K384 author specimens reproduced 192 of 192. ## Arithmetic, commitments and public terminal data

Both keys use Goldilocks and the quadratic extension `K=Fp²`, a trace subgroup of size `n=4096`, LDE coset size `N=131072`, 135 witness columns (60 routed), four fixed selector/constant columns and 60 fixed permutation sigma columns. There are two selectors with groups [0,5) and [5,7), no lookup table or lookup polynomial. The gates in order are Noop, Constant(2), PublicInput, BaseSum2(59), Arithmetic(15), Poseidon2, and original Poseidon. Their declared degrees are 0,1,1,2,3,7,7. Selector filtering raises the maximum allowed degree to nine. The key's 123 is the maximum vector length of the gate evaluator's constraints, not a count of 123 arithmetic operations or 123 rows. [Decoded keys and actual descriptors](KEYS.json) · [Selector grouping, lines 81–145](https://github.com/Quantus-Network/qp-plonky2/blob/632f23ca07712bfa5bc8ed082bc05c41c5929dfb/plonky2/src/gates/selectors.rs) · [Gate definitions](https://github.com/Quantus-Network/qp-plonky2/tree/632f23ca07712bfa5bc8ed082bc05c41c5929dfb/verifier/src/gates)

For each of two beta/gamma pairs, the permutation product traverses routed wires in groups of at most eight with seven within-row partial-product polynomials, fixes Z at the first row, and relates Z(x) to Z(gx). The verifier concatenates **both** sets of boundary and product identities with the filtered gate constraints, then reduces this complete vector twice with distinct base-field alpha powers. There are two quotients, each eight chunks of degree <n. For each repetition it checks `V(z) = (z^n−1) Σ_j t_j(z) z^(nj)` at the **same** extension point z. For fixed degree-bounded oracle polynomials, the residual degree is <9n, including a possibly dishonest degree-<8n quotient times the vanishing polynomial. If an individual residual is nonzero and z uniform and independent, its probability of coincident evaluation is at most `9n/|K|`, about 2^-112.83. This bound conditions on fixed low-degree data and a nonzero residual. Two equations at the same point do not square it. The honest prover excludes a z in its trace subgroup. The challenger draws from K without rejection, so a reduction sampling outside a prescribed domain must reconcile the exceptional event explicitly. ## Exact Fiat–Shamir schedule

Both `Hasher` and `InnerHasher` are original Goldilocks Poseidon. A 12-word state has rate eight, capacity four, S-box x⁷, eight full and 22 partial rounds. The statement digest absorbs its fixed count of public fields via the no-pad hash. The fixed cap contributes to the registered circuit digest. Merkle leaves initialize capacity word 8 to leaf length + 1. Binary internal nodes absorb the eight ordered digest limbs into zero capacity. The challenger overwrites rate lanes, permutes when full or when squeezing pending inputs, discards buffered outputs on a new input, and pops remaining squeezed words in reverse. Each cap contains 16 four-word digests. Each digest ranges over p⁴ possibilities. [Hasher config, lines 84–89](https://github.com/Quantus-Network/qp-plonky2/blob/632f23ca07712bfa5bc8ed082bc05c41c5929dfb/core/src/config.rs) · [Merkle leaf/hash code, lines 47–84 and 150–166](https://github.com/Quantus-Network/qp-plonky2/blob/632f23ca07712bfa5bc8ed082bc05c41c5929dfb/core/src/hashing.rs) · [Challenger, lines 28–139](https://github.com/Quantus-Network/qp-plonky2/blob/632f23ca07712bfa5bc8ed082bc05c41c5929dfb/core/src/challenger.rs)

1. Absorb `[5,4,0,1,2,3,26,1,12,2,2,2,2,2]` (FRI config, strategy and reduction geometry), four-word circuit digest and four-word PI hash. Absorb the 16-digest wire cap. Draw two base betas then two base gammas.
2. Absorb the 16-digest Z+partials cap. Draw two base alphas. Absorb the quotient cap, draw one quadratic z. No lookup coins.
3. Absorb the evaluation batches in challenge order. At z these are constants (4), sigmas (60), W (135), Z (2), partials (14), quotient (16), hence 231 extension values. Then absorb the two next-point Zs. The contiguous wire encoding places next-point Zs earlier. Reordering matters. Draw one extension FRI alpha to batch the quotient-of-openings checks.
4. Five times absorb one 16-digest FRI cap, then draw one extension beta to fold four values. Domains proceed 131072 → 32768 → 8192 → 2048 → 512 → 128. Final degree bound is <4. Absorb all four final extension coefficients and one base PoW witness. Draw one base response, with zero grinding bits, then 26 base words modulo N, with replacement. For a uniform base word the reduction has one residue with one extra representative (`p mod N = 1`). At each position the proof discloses all four initial oracle leaves and all five folded leaves with binary authentication paths. ## What the K384 replacement changes

The build overlays the registered profiles with a replacement native argument configuration and 64 queries. It retains all monetary source constraints and `InnerHasher`. K3M1 and K3L1 have 16,384 trace rows, 524,288 initial positions, six four-way folds and four terminal coefficients. The selector/gate polynomial degree, Fp² challenge size, two parallel PLONK repetitions, random no-op/copy-pair mechanism and unsalted folded leaves persist. The larger trace raises the isolated shared-z residual upper bound to `9 × 16384 / p²` (2^-110.83). The fixed-agreement quarter example becomes 2^-128, and the half-agreement example 2^-64, with the same adaptive/list caveats. Costing an unchanged 128-bit extension as 128 quantum work ignores both the relation degree and the compiler. Internal nodes include exactly two ordered 48-byte digests. For the transcript each fixed 48-byte digest maps injectively into seven little-endian field limbs of 7,7,7,7,7,7,6 bytes. A fresh SHAKE256 invocation absorbs the FS tag plus **all 16** canonical state words, then rejection-samples 16 field words. The library challenger overwrites the first eight rate words and squeezes eight. This maps a state to XOF output. It is not an invertible permutation by construction. Fixed hash inputs have useful framing and wider 384-bit collision scale (ideal quantum queries 2^128, classical 2^192), yet a proof that this salted-cap, stateful, multi-round construction realizes the programmable QROM compiler remains missing. ## Reproduce the public checks

Run from the repository root with Python 3. The key-only check consumes the two committed keys. Both release specimen directories contain `ordinary.bin` and `swap.bin`. The baseline vectors are from the independent funded comparison run. The candidate vectors are from the matched K384 author cycle, including its onward ordinary payment. The `core` argument is the Rust crate directory containing `src/poseidon.rs`.
