# Retain the live spend proof for the devnet. Defer a production selection.

Keep MNG1 for transfers and supply operations and SLG1 for each authorized SWP1 leg. K384 removed a specific generic collision bottleneck in the **proof's Merkle commitments** and successfully spent the same funded note types. Its complete ordinary payment and each signed swap frame exceed the active 262,144-byte limit, including after transcript-derived compression. K384 also retains the unresolved proof-of-knowledge and hiding assumptions. A switch would increase cost without establishing the target. Keep K384 as an executable comparator and pursue a funded-note frontend for a different proof. The next **product** milestone remains token-for-native atomic swaps. ## Evidence that determines the choice

A non-author ran the baseline on the mined local network from revision `3f08bff0089096d7fb27279bc0d444006e9147ce`. The selected H27 history had 70 fixed units, 330 managed units, 2,700 cumulative native subsidy and 36 fees. The two owners exchanged 7 fixed for 30 managed, restored their wallets and spent the actual receipts. The clean challenger build and new wallets used public executable revision `2a3a4c4671af54dd7d1171c77b826339d12d8cbf`. Its isolated fixture verified historical MNG1-authorized funding, a signed managed register/mint and holder burn, and matched the real asset, amount, input count, change and single native fee. Two isolated wallet roles opened both incoming outputs and both change outputs. The comparison driver checked their responses against the agreed terms and obtained two distinct signatures over the same complete message. The live baseline additionally exercised the ordinary negotiation journal and both local authorization commands. Subsequent restored-wallet spending crossed both proof profiles. The challenger fixture supplies synthetic anchors. It performs no PoW admission. ## Which security claims survive

- **Spend authority and inflation.** Both circuits check opening knowledge for each real input, hidden membership under the public anchor, claimed nullifiers, typed output commitments and bounded 62-bit quantities. The per-asset and native equations have integer interpretations within the verified ranges. Consensus checks current branch eligibility, nullifier uniqueness, authorized supply deltas and atomic admission; swap settlement also checks two authorizations and one total fee. A holder burn and an issuer mint require different authorizations. For a *satisfied relation* these are identifiable controls. Inferring possession of the witness from an arbitrary accepted noninteractive proof still requires a knowledge argument for this instantiation. A public proof of a supply delta alone cannot mint. [Circuit and theorem map](assurance/REVIEW.md) · [Independent monetary checks](review/check_candidate.py)
- **Classical and quantum estimates.** The baseline uses original Poseidon for the argument hashes and challenger, Poseidon2 for funded notes and membership, Goldilocks with a quadratic challenge field, 26 FRI queries, rate 1/32 and zero grinding. K384 uses SHA3-384 argument commitments, a native SHAKE256-based state update and 64 queries; the original algebraic public-input hash and funded-note commitments remain. For an ideal arbitrary collision the output-width comparison moves from roughly 85 to 128 quantum query bits on the argument commitments. Fixed-target theft and general proof forgery are different games. At a fixed quarter agreement the two conditional sampling terms have exponents near 52 and 128 before compiler and list losses. The security target is 128 bits of quantum work on the *complete* system, with adaptive provers and recorded receipts. Neither experiment establishes it. [Target and adversary](TARGET.md) · [Concrete bounds](candidate/BOUNDS.md) · [Quantum collision result](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.05385)
- **Transcript and witness hiding.** An independent replay checked every derived query position against the registered preprocessing cap: 78/78 across the baseline specimens and 192/192 for K384. Both use row blinding and salted initial oracles; extension-field evaluations, later unsalted FRI openings and the terminal polynomial are public. A proof of joint hiding for the actual transcript, including its correlated messages and repetitions, remains open. Applying a generic ideal-QROM transformation requires the matching round-by-round knowledge argument, HVZK and compatible oracles; soundness alone does not imply hiding or an extractor. The chip-level cost of a realistic quantum adversary was not measured. [Full transcript, constants, reductions and boundary of each cited result](assurance/REVIEW.md) · [Ideal compiler, Theorems 3 and 8.6](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/834)
- **Recipient privacy.** The actual wallet uses reusable ML-KEM-1024 receiving keys and two ChaCha20-Poly1305 ciphertexts per encapsulation for the note and memo. The six observed receipts were distinct; recovery and spend succeeded. The public swap exposes its two linked sides, payer, expiry, output roles and total fee. Counterparties know the terms. Reused nullifiers and identical retries are public links. End-to-end key privacy, network anonymity, malicious-sender receipt correctness and long-term privacy after static key compromise are distinct obligations. [Receipt field map and observations](RECEIVING.md) · [ML-KEM standard](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/203/final)

## What could change the decision

The target needs an explicit extraction and hiding result for the complete verifier and a concrete budget for the implemented noninteractive compiler. Under the ideal BCS bound of order `t²ε + t³/2^λ`, even granting interactive error 2^-130 cannot justify 2^128 quantum queries. Raising the Merkle output to 384 bits changes the collision term only. A future parameter choice needs slack for constants, multiple attempts, loss through relation batching and the wider system. For the present four-field funded hashes and 256-bit transaction/signature digests, distinguish an arbitrary collision from control over a funded target. Versioned note rules may be necessary for a stronger claim. [Primary theorem map](assurance/REVIEW.md) · [Existing funds and activation policy](FUNDED_NOTES.md)

**Next proof experiment.** Complete a verifier-owned Goldilocks R1CS frontend for one funded MNG1 transfer and both SLG1 legs, using the tested legacy parent-hash lowering. First compare all 31/34 public fields, accepted and rejected assignments, current nullifiers, typed value/fee conservation and output receipts with the approved native verifiers. The new test already obtained the same parent root as the native code with 2,536 constraints. Only after that parity check, register a masked WHIR or constrained-code configuration with explicit round-by-round extraction, joint hiding, subfield, terminal opening and ideal-to-concrete hash assumptions. Price the compiled error before proving; if a required premise fails, identify that premise and separate any need for a new note version. Measure a complete payment and both fully signed frames against the existing cap. The older successful CP03 WHIR proofs cover a different native-only relation and cannot be counted as this result. The blockchain's global research objective remains active.
