Keep Goldilocks quadratic extension, two challenges, 1/32 rate, cap height 4 and two-bit FRI folds; raise FRI queries from 26 to 64. The pinned Qnero gadget builder exports qp-plonky2's fixed D=2 alias. Discriminator: remove the original native Poseidon oracle and TKQ1's 256-bit generic hash-output collision ceiling for proof Merkle commitments. In an ideal black-box single hash with a fixed 384-bit output, constant-success generic collision queries scale as 2^192 classically and 2^128 in the quantum query model. This is neither an instantiated SHA3 attack cost nor a spend forgery bound. Increasing to 64 queries reduces the ideal fixed-quarter-agreement event from 2^-52 to 2^-128, before Fiat–Shamir adaptivity, list sizes, salt/hiding, repetitions, target multiplicity or concrete quantum loss. TKQ1 used Keccak-256, an eight-word-rate/four-word-capacity challenger and 64 queries for the historical fixed-token relation. This experiment changes the argument oracles to permit genuine same-note compatibility and measured complete transactions. The larger verifier registries and packets belong to an isolated experiment. No new network rule or relaxation of the live 262144-byte physical frame is authorized.
