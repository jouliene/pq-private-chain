# Private note architecture decision

**Retain MNG1/SLG1 for funded devnet operations. Keep N7P1 as an executable, isolated research pool. Reject the priced N3B1 encoding for this network.** N7P1 has a real funded 2×2 transfer, seed-restored onward spend and two-owner atomic swap. Its verifier enforces a precise occurrence predicate that the live circuit lacks. The available evidence justifies that narrower engineering claim. It leaves open 128-bit post-quantum spend knowledge and privacy of the complete payment. The measured verifier rebuilding its circuit for each public statement also exceeds the home-node CPU scenario. Existing users have no conversion transaction and their funds stay under the original rules. [Runnable walkthrough](README.md) · [Independent technical review](review/REVIEW.md)

## What changed at the funding boundary

The live node validates an eligible branch header and current spentness. Its real-slot arithmetic verifies a path to the root inside that header, with a privately chosen path depth from 0 to 20. The pinned verifier does not compare that choice with the authentic leaf count, enforce a funded index below that count, or require the wallet generator's sorting of children. Its owner, opening, native amount, token lane and deterministic nullifier are still constrained. Finding an arbitrary pair of colliding parents alone supplies neither a valid alternative funded opening nor a spending credential. The structural fixed-target concern for the four-capacity-word public permutation deserves its own analysis even for the live padded parent. It does not establish a forged payment. Ordinary, fixed-target and chosen-root games, and what each source actually proves, are distinguished in the [live source audit](baseline/LIVE.md).

The physical proof-only size for the live transfer and signed swap leg is **139,378 B**. Its serialized proof fields are 139,634 and 139,658 B respectively. The complete transfer is 143,233 B, the signed leg 160,446 B and a two-owner bundle 320,905 B. The first historical FTL1 leg carried 238,120 proof bytes, 98,742 more than SLG1. An independent parse of both previously published funding fixtures reconciles the byte counts and all vector prefixes. The historical maximum signed FTL1 frame remains 261,068 B. ## Funded evidence

The experiment creates four independent 48-byte backups and seven transparent, authorized genesis credits on a new PoW history. The setup pins the complete founding identity outside the block directory. Each public verifier mounts that trust anchor, branch files, registered verifier and runtime. Each wallet additionally sees precisely its own seed and private working directory. A descriptor carries a new format and founding key scope. Outputs commit to their private opening. After ML-KEM decapsulation an honest recipient checks the authenticated plaintext against its own owner ID and the public commitment. A deliberately bad receipt can leave the sender's funds unclaimable. It cannot credit the honest recipient.

1. Two positive Alice inputs carrying (native, A) = (5,11) and (7,23) pay offline Bob (4,13) and return (5,21) after a native fee of 3.
2. A new Bob process starts with one backup, an empty wallet directory and public history. It recovers the received note plus its separate native-only genesis credit of 8. The prover consumes both and pays (5,3) onward, returning (4,10) as change.
3. Independently isolated Carol and Dana prove separate legs and open both promised receipts before approving a transfer of 7 A for 30 B. Dana pays the single three-unit fee. The script checks each final wallet independently. Four proofs, eight distinct spent nullifiers and three mined packets leave 37 native units including 9 credited to the miner, 48 A and 35 B. Each selected branch rejects duplicate output commitments. The index-based NF is stable on that branch. Re-inclusion of the same commitment at a different position on another fork changes its NF. A deployment would need branch-scoped rollback/recovery.
4. Whole-block validation rejects a duplicate spend, altered receipt, altered proof, foreign network, wrong count/root, incomplete swap and altered final signature without advancing committed state. Separate witness tests change membership, index, spend secret, duplicate occurrence, asset, range and conservation; changed public statements include the fee. Public-only verification rebuilds the branch root from the actual blocks. The old and new encoding domains remain disjoint and the old funded pool has no right to authorize a new-pool debit. [Funded runner](lab/run.py) · [Wallet and authorization](lab/wallet.py) · [Public rules](lab/state.py) · [Witness controls](candidate/check.py)

Only the genesis grants and deliberately disclosed aggregate walkthrough balances are public test diagnostics. Normal packets expose slot count, NFs, commitments, ordered receipt bytes, pool/network, root/count, fee and transaction type. A swap adds its side, fee payer, expiry, common terms hash, distinct ephemeral verification keys and public pairing. The peers learn the negotiated amounts and opposite addresses. Reusing an input at the same position links conflicting attempts by its NF. Traffic timing and static-key compromise can reveal recipients. Of the four funded owners only three initially have two inputs. Tree depth does not measure actual anonymity. The fixture has one item per block and accepts the current tree root/count only. Concurrent disjoint offers at the same tip can invalidate one another. The existing devnet's retained-anchor feature would need a deliberate port. A lone new recipient needs a second positive note in this two-real-input profile. No faucet, bridge, general issuance, secret-sharing coordinator or production consensus is implicit here.

## Lifetime and assurance

The target is an adversary with quantum computation and oracle access, adaptive funding, access to eligible public branches and history, and repeated, correlated proofs and receipts. Separate adversarial games cover false value creation, spending an honest owner's occurrence, consuming the same note twice, and distinguishing honest recipients/amounts at an otherwise equal public trace. Static seed compromise and an actively disclosing counterparty fall outside the equal-trace privacy comparison. Model 20 years, 100M included operations/year and a 90% transfer / 10% swap mix for sizing: 4.4 billion output occurrences, at most two billion post-genesis roots for this one-item block policy, and just one eligible root at a time. A long-lived recipient can still be targeted across many roots or many notes. A conditional failure bound per attempted statement must also be aggregated over adaptive attempts, distinct from counting notes.

* **N3B1.** In an ideal random function SHA3-384's generic scales are 128 quantum query bits for a freely chosen collision and 192 for one preimage. Searching 4.4 billion comparable targets has a scale of about 176 bits. The concrete SHA3 permutation and the full receipt protocol have different security obligations. DFMS's elementwise commit-and-open Theorem 4.2 yields the stated *conditional extraction* failure below 2^-64.67 at the selected quantum query budget, with 512-bit oracle commitments, specified labels and challenge encoding. Applying a union bound over 2^40 individually covered games yields less than 2^-24.67. Neither number bounds lifetime joint privacy of real transactions. [DFMS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.13730) · [Exact assumptions and derivation](sha3/PROOF.md)
* **N7P1.** Seven Goldilocks digest words enlarge the idealized output collision exponent from 85.3 to 149.3. The latter is a generic work scale, **not** a lower bound for unauthorized spending. A fixed-target public-permutation construction, keyed nullifier, tagged additive field absorption and cross-block squeeze need their own analysis under known Poseidon2 parameters. The 384-bit backup and 256-bit DEM key have different exhaustible spaces. The full statement uses SHA3-384 specialization, the FRI uses 32 queries, a quartic extension and SHAKE-based challenges. Two PLONK challenges, configured “128”, row blinding and leaf salts do not supply a quantum same-witness extractor or a simulator for the joint opening vector, fold caps, terminal polynomial and correlated envelopes. Under hypothetical independent 1/4 acceptance per query, the query factor alone is 2^-64 before other losses. [Poseidon2](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/323) · [Quantum ideal sponge games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16887) · [Multi-round Fiat–Shamir](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/282) · [Reed–Solomon proximity](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/654)
* **Receiving.** FIPS 203 standardizes the KEM. It does not prove key anonymity/robustness for maliciously correlated keys, recipient hiding in a payment conditioned on its hash and later NF, or forward secrecy after seed exposure. SLH-DSA-SHAKE-128f provides a separately specified authorization primitive. No classical EC assumption is needed for these receipts. [FIPS 203](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/203/final) · [FIPS 205](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/205/final) · [Review and lifetime table](review/REVIEW.md)

## Measured cost and decision threshold

Four funded proofs on an i7-8700K, four Rayon threads, produced 226,816 / 237,092 / 231,235 / 233,781 B. The two ordinary wire packets were **230,657 and 240,933 B**. The signed swap legs were **252,249 and 254,795 B**, the bundle **507,056 B** and its block **507,236 B**. Each mined block adds 180 B. Negotiation in the harness exchanged two proof-carrying cores of 235,161 and 237,707 B, two 17,088 B approvals, plus a 7,311 B canonical private agreement if sent once. These figures count logical messages per transfer between parties. A wallet relaying a fully assembled packet to the miner adds the whole bundle again. No transport, retry or gossip deduplication savings were measured.

A legal N7P1 proof can grow to 246,120 B. The signed maximum exceeds the old frame by 4,990 B. The isolated node applies a **272 KiB** cap, 11,394 B above that maximum. Raising a physical slot cap from 256 to 272 KiB allows another 16 KiB per full slot, 1 MiB for a 64-frame queue, about 6.55 ms per full frame at 20 Mbps, and 64 KiB more for four slots. A pair may use two slots. The real product cap is untouched. Retrying until a sampled proof happens to fit would alter latency, observer linkage and possibly signatures. The 93/100,000 overflow frequency is an independent sample of IID query vectors, not measured wallet failure probability. [Independent model and limits](review/REVIEW.md)

Cold recovery in this implementation revalidates the whole selected history and trial-opens every receipt. The measured prefix for Bob was 29,764 B of genesis JSON plus 230,837 B of first block. With a wholly retained chain the modeled 20-year N7P1 scan reads about 526 TB before forks/replication. A hypothetical compact scan with one kind byte, four 56 B digests and two 1,744 B receipts per leg is 3,713 B/leg, 8.17 TB over 20 years, before authentication, framing, special credits and synchronization state. It needs a separately trusted or authenticated complete stream. Pruning a full node saves old proof bodies only when some other source serves cold clients. Raw NFs, leaves and completed tree parents alone cost about 36.96 GB/year at this mix, before database indices, commitment uniqueness, block headers, optional fork history and recovery data. The measured Python ledger retains complete history and copies its in-memory tree layers per block. No production pruned store or sustained throughput was measured.

## Next decision

Advance **one fixed verification key with 49 verifier-owned public rows** as a bounded component-to-payment experiment. Replace per-statement compilation with a single registered PLONK key; bind every public field in Fiat–Shamir and check the public-row term in the quotient. Independent interpolation tests match direct polynomial evaluation at 16,384 rows and detect every one-word statement change. The isolated quartic evaluator took a median 2.36 ms in Python. A straightforward prover adds up to 1,228,800 FFT butterflies and ~1 MiB temporary evaluation space. Zero added proof bytes depends on the resulting trace dimension and degree. This has not produced a reusable-key payment. The decisive run must build the actual fixed-key circuit once, produce a complete funded proof, vary all 49 fields and charge cold setup, warm public verification, quotient degree, blinding and end-to-end admission at mixed load. It cannot by itself establish quantum knowledge, widen the privacy set, solve historical data availability or reduce 26 TB/year to 5 TB/year. Require separate review of the defined sponge, FRI transcript and receipt composition before considering funded migration. [Costed alternative and measured algebraic check](candidate/alternative/README.md)

The isolated experiment permits no imports, shared nullifiers, dual registration, balance migration or old-proof credits. Any later migration requires a versioned, atomic consume-old/create-new rule with a shared accounting ledger, original spend authority and unambiguous spent identity under reorganization. The live chain and the global private PoW project continue independently.
