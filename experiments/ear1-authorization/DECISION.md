# Park EAR1 pending a funded-witness extraction result

**Decision of November 22, 2026.** Keep C on the funded PoW chain and SGM1 as the executable reference. Park EAR1 and the full-prefix compiler candidate for external cryptographic review. Chiesa, Di, Hu and Zheng give an adaptive knowledge theorem for a particular noninteractive compiler. Its extractor cannot read its required commitment tuple from EAR1's very first degree-challenge query. Replacing the compiler removes that interface mismatch. A proof that its underlying interactive protocol yields the funded witnesses with adequate probability is still missing. The present evidence supports neither mainnet authorization at 128 bits nor a demonstration of an unauthorized spend. ## The monetary decision

A success for the adversary is an **accepted unauthorized debit** of a designated honest funded occurrence on an eligible branch, after choosing a statement and observing permitted public history. Count each spend once across competing versions using current nullifier state. Replaying an authorized payment, paying with one's own funded inputs, or producing an arbitrary hash collision does not meet this event. To test the hardest two-real-input case we use distinct owner secrets. A witness must open both input commitments, supply both ASK preimages and valid hidden positions and paths to the verifier's root, satisfy the nullifier and per-asset equations, and authorize the receipt-bound intent. Owner-key derivation, commitment and path binding, eligible branch selection, atomic current spentness, issuance, and both parties' final signatures for swaps remain separate assumptions or checks. The proof alone cannot identify the legal holder of an equivalent cryptographic opening. * **Published proof.** We reverified the 192,160-byte funded EAR1 payment against its signed count-13 prefix. Its 188,170-byte proof opens 60 of 4,096 salted columns and supplies its 22,650-byte degree table before the first GKR fold. SHAKE receives a rolling state. Theorem 11.3's Construction 11.9 needs to parse commitments from the input to a superposed challenge query, including the full prefix in later rounds. A deterministic classical replay of the wallet transcript cannot perform this operation for an adaptive quantum prover. The short clear rounds and the final ring-switch opening need their own correspondence too. [Primary theorem mapping](THEOREM.md) · [Registered trace](../sgm1-security/evidence/EAR1_TRACE.json)
* **What the decoder actually recovered.** A fresh signed funding prefix and two independent test secrets produced the real circuit assignment. We supplied 152 authenticated columns from its test-owned commitment. The decoder used 151 to interpolate, checked the extra column and recovered exactly the 6,155-byte witness, including both secrets and the two funded occurrences. Changing either recovered secret failed the monetary predicate. At the degree layer alone, the test also constructed two assignments agreeing at 60 positions and agreeing on every basis value of the early degree table while differing elsewhere. Those assignments need not share a Merkle root or pass the terminal. This establishes the sufficiency of the supplied data for this honest fixture. Obtaining sufficient consistent data in the adaptive proof game is the unresolved step. [Executable](decode_probe.py) · [Result](decoder.json)
* **Where the reduction stops after changing the wire.** The candidate commits anew at all 215 rounds and gives the verifier explicit inputs and outputs for every Merkle check. Conditional on a whole-relation relaxed round-by-round knowledge argument, the CDHZ multi-extractor supplies partial strings and the state-restoration extractor transports an accepting terminal witness backwards to the money witness. The earliest unresolved interactive premise is the state before the degree challenge: it must cover sparse or inconsistent partial columns and a degree table chosen after the challenge. It must also compose with every masked GKR fold, scalar row, quotient and Artin–Schreier terminal. No theorem matched here supplies that extractor and error for this IOR. ## Work and success budget

Use one adaptive adversary with a **total of at most 2^128 superposition queries** to the ideal compiler oracle family, arbitrary adaptively selected payments and a target debit with probability one half. Reserve one quarter for extraction error to leave a constant probability of recovering a witness. These are research accounting choices. A bound on ideal queries does not specify physical gates, qubit memory, time to implement a SHAKE query or CPU time to run the extractor. There is no concrete extraction RAM bound for this money protocol. Honest historic proofs and auxiliary observations must be simulated or independently justified. Treating them as free advice correlated with the oracle could invalidate the reduction. Concrete primitive security at the resulting extractor work remains an additional obligation. [Game and extractor work](THEOREM.md)

With 215 rounds, the leading error multiplier at this query budget is about 320 × 2^256. Inserting a hypothetical per-round error of 2^-256 gives about **320**. A probability upper estimate above one provides no assurance about success. The single-budget quarter-slack calculation calls for a proved error of approximately **2^-266.322 or smaller**, hence 267 whole bits. A separate union scenario with 2.2 billion legs and a conditional per-event adversary budget requires 298 whole bits. This is a different lifetime model. The corrected salted-tree term at 640 ideal output bits is about 2^-243.43 and the finite-challenge term about 2^-118.25 for one global game. Widening just the tree hash cannot fix the interactive error. A 640-bit SHAKE256 output still uses its 512-bit-capacity sponge. ## Privacy and the external-review handoff

The ledger publishes anchor, fee, nullifiers, commitments, encrypted receipts, and the atomic association of two swap legs. Identical retries are linkable; competing spends reuse nullifiers. A negotiating counterparty knows its negotiated amounts and descriptors, and the coordinator observes draft exchange, ordering and retries. The earlier matched corpus had no shared public nullifier, ciphertext or commitment between the selected receipt and its honest onward spend. That observation measures only those equality tests. A joint simulator for the actual masks, early degree table, terminal, receipt encryption, authorized counterparties, adaptive retries and later spends remains open. CDHZ knowledge extraction supplies no such simulator. [Observed metadata](../sgm1-security/evidence/OBSERVER.json) · [Wallet and threat boundary](../sgm1-security/wallet.md)

**Reopen on this result:** a reviewer supplies a whole-relation adaptive state-restoration bound directly, or a relaxed round-by-round knowledge lemma with explicit prefix state, extractors for both funded openings and concrete error after composing the custom terminal. It must price all oracle-accessible history and the actual extractor's work, or identify a different monetary reduction with a tighter budget. A successful honest decoder, a smaller frame or an additional throughput run cannot settle that question. [Independent reproduction instructions](README.md)
