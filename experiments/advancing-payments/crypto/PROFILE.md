# Advancing private payments, APV2 research profile

Registration must precede proving. The suite is a new local protocol based on DigitalGuards/qnero f04c91307837c0231668383bec76ba98d89d2652 and locked qp-plonky2 1.5.5. For **all** leaf, wallet and outer circuits set security_bits 128, Goldilocks extension D=2, two challenges, 43 FRI queries, rate bits 3, cap height 4 and zero grinding. These are parameters and conjectural screening terms, not measured 128-bit classical or PQ security. The leaf (143 wires, 80 routed) is not hiding, and always remains inside its wallet. The width-1 private batch and each outer wrapper (135 wires, 60 routed) are row-blinded. Every non-singleton wrapper fixes its arity and respective inner verifier as constants; distinct eligible headers are permitted. Each outer registers an APV2 tag, arity and all 26 fields per wallet in submission order.

The registrar deterministically builds the leaf and four admission circuits from installed source, prior to private funding or proving. It emits exact common and circuit-specific verifier-only artifacts, their combined byte encodings and BLAKE2b-512 hashes. The deployment operator independently registers and pins the resulting directory, verifies it before mounting read-only, and permits only the registered keys and software. User/peer packets never supply a verifier, circuit, parameter choice or private program. Wallet and builder compare their actual fresh compiled circuit data to the deployment registration. The public verifier consumes only this local registration and public bytes. This profile does not use the original Qnero release's 100-bit verifier allowlist or claim acceptance by that release.

Goldilocks note and tree rules, fixed 1,792-byte suite-1 ciphertexts with ML-KEM-1024/ChaCha20-Poly1305 and 62-bit amounts are inherited; each real transaction spends two locally opened funded notes, creates two independently encrypted notes and pays the exact research fee of three units. Every public slot contains a nonzero pair of commitments, two distinct nullifiers, height and anchor, fee and ordered ciphertext digest. Ledger history approval, lookup against recent anchors, uniqueness across state, bounded cumulative value, atomic application, header construction and branch/recovery rules belong to the separate state implementation. A fresh proof that conflicts with previously accepted notes must pass verification and fail state admission. A synthetic genesis issuer does not imply real issuance or public funding privacy.

Transport frames are u32le length-prefixed for the research local pipe. The consensus candidate packet is APV2 || arity:u8 || proof_bytes:u32le || canonical proof || 3584*arity ciphertext bytes. Only arities 1, 2, 3, 4 are registered. Hash operations H/R/N and the wallet P/S commands are local RPCs, not network privacy services. Wallet selection indexes are not sent to peers. Receipt recovery scans authenticated common history, both ciphertexts per slot, their corresponding commitments, and the common public spent-nullifier set. The private tmpfs backup is only the 32-byte wallet seed. Restored keys cannot regenerate the synthetic random genesis openings; recovery and subsequent spending exercise actual received notes.

The P command carries its private local selector (u16le) and the 132-byte public header, followed by a stream of commitment pages and then the receiver address. S takes one stream of ordered (commitment, ciphertext) pages and then a separate public nullifier stream. Each stream terminates on an empty frame and each nonempty page contains whole records, at most 256 KiB. The laboratory implementation materializes these streams in process memory. Qnero's complete tree is rebuilt locally at each spend. The builder emits one framed READY after cold setup, before accepting any groups. The proof relation allows zero-value dummy slots; this campaign's wallet constructor explicitly opens two funded notes and calls InputNote::real for each.

Build with an offline-populated Cargo cache, a clean disposable checkout at the pinned revision and rustup toolchain 1.93.0. The runner applies profile.patch, compiles one release example with two build jobs, checks source restoration and emits the executable and build identity.

```sh
python3 experiments/advancing-payments/crypto/build.py QNERO_CHECKOUT CARGO_TARGET_DIR OUTPUT_BINARY
```

No dependency revisions are changed. The accepted circuit configurations are defined in profile.patch, registry.rs validates them before recording a registration, and the operator pins the whole generated key directory. The unused upstream public-batch and optional standalone ZK leaf constructors retain their release parameters. Neither is instantiated by APV2.
