# Scheduled direct spending of funded notes

The local experiment retains the released empty AST1 genesis, network ID, block format and PoW. It changes the spend argument that validators will accept for the same monetary relation. Funding is mined by the released MNG1/SLG1 deployment before the upgraded software starts. The operator installs the new code, schedule and verification keys. A transaction cannot supply a verifier. Parameter activation is a coordinated hard fork. Legacy validators can stay on a different chain if they never adopt it.

| Candidate block height | Zero-delta ordinary spend and signed swap | Supply operations |
|---|---|---|
| 1–12 | A | Released AST1/MNG1 policy |
| 13–14 | A or B, both swap seats in one profile | Released AST1/MNG1 policy |
| 15 onward | B | Released AST1/MNG1 policy |

Height refers to the candidate's parent and branch. Mempool admission and template construction must use the next candidate height. A locally constructed proof and the date the wallet last synced confer no eligibility. Detachment reverses the branch-local spends, coins, fees and indexed occurrences. On reconnection the node reapplies policy at each block's own height. A replaying node has the schedule for both eras.

## Why use a new proof profile

B increases FRI sampling from 26 to 32 rounds. Reading the new registered keys shows that the blinded trace also crosses a power-of-two boundary, growing from 4,096 to 8,192 rows. Each key has the original public fields, five four-way folds and a rate of 1/32. The terminal polynomial doubles from four to eight quadratic-extension coefficients. The ordinary and independently owned swap relations retain their native authorization, conservation and receipt equations. Both the transcript and the verified openings thus change. For these fixed encodings, the public packet and proof lengths can be calculated before any private prover runs. [Key and framing calculator](profile.py)

N7P2 uses different funded commitments, nullifiers, receipt encryption and a different backup layout. Its successful new-genesis experiment supplies no equality proof from an MNG1 opening to an N7P2 output, nor a cross-pool spentness rule. Adding that bridge would introduce additional monetary trust. Direct spending under compatible B measures the smaller change.

If the fraction of acceptable indices of a fixed oracle were exactly one quarter and the samples independent, the query-only term would move from approximately 2^-52 to 2^-64. FRI folding/list bounds, Fiat–Shamir extraction, quantum oracle access, adaptive choices, and the composition of commitments, nullifiers, proofs and reusable-key receipts require further arguments. Extra openings also bear on blinding and joint privacy. This experiment has no quantitative end-to-end security gain to assign. The MNG2 native circuit still includes the five public supply words. The outer B verifier accepts exactly the transfer operation and authenticated zero delta. Conversely the A exception after height 14 requires a genuine nonzero authorized supply operation. It can consume native funds, pay a recipient or return change. The schedule retires the ordinary and swap encodings while preserving this economic authority.

A validating node carries all ledger effects accepted under A into B, including historic supply. B cannot retrospectively remove a forged historical mint or repair weak ownership, a compromised wallet seed or operator-approved invalid history. Ordinary recipients retain the old receipt-key and multi-target hash assumptions. Public observers see the proof version and heights, commitment and nullifier lists, fee and ciphertexts. Re-proposing the same real spend under another version links both attempts by its nullifier; which original output it spent requires other information. Swap frames additionally reveal the pairing, output roles, payer and expiry. A confirmed direct spend requires no special migration pair of public debit and credit. The published amounts are intentional disclosures for this disposable test.
