# Keep MNG1 and SLG1 on the local PoW devnet

Directly checking Poseidon2 seventh powers makes a packed TCitH spend small enough to submit. At the first party-domain size that fits a signed leg, it raises the modeled mixed archive from **16.11 to 26.48 TB/year** at 100M operations. A measured wallet commitment for one leg processes **6.24 GB** of field shares. More importantly, the interactive extraction and privacy arguments do not establish 128-bit quantum knowledge security or joint privacy for the noninteractive funded transaction. The evidence supports retaining the deployed verifiers and deferring both replacements. Previously funded users keep their receipts, keys, spend authority and nullifiers. ## What was actually exercised

Starting from `d92de79fe1fce97a33d88f706996e6cdd8ec188f`, the funding experiment replayed a real mined branch in isolated wallet roles. It generated four fresh **reference** proofs, for an ordinary transfer, two funded swap-leg statements and a restored owner's onward spend. It ran each exact private assignment through both frozen encodings, matched the 31 or 34 public field elements and the ordered receipt digest, and checked every linear wiring equation, nonlinear port and padded coordinate. The onward wallet opened its 8-unit output from the original mined swap and constructed a spend of 5 plus fee 3. The two newly generated leg assignments are tested statements; a new exchange was not jointly signed or mined in this experiment. Only the disclosed test totals and public validation results leave the private runs. Wallet openings, path positions and siblings remain private. ## Security decision and next experiment

There is a concrete component-level reason to study TCitH. The live proof uses original Poseidon argument hashes, a quadratic Goldilocks challenge field, 26 FRI queries, rate 1/32 and zero grinding. The often-cited 130-bit product is a configuration screen. TCitH supplies a finite combinatorial interactive bound and replaces the proof's roughly 256-bit commitment outputs with 512-bit hashes. In an ideal arbitrary-collision game that changes the generic quantum query exponent for those digests from about 85 to 171. Spending a specified funded note is a different game; the note and joint-signature hashes retain their old domains. [Live verifier parameters and hash games](../spend-proof-decision/DECISION.md)

The 128-bit work target covers an adaptive quantum forger with superposition hash queries, classical wallet/ledger access, and a recorder of receipts, swaps, retries and forks. Classical interactive extraction in the TCitH construction returns a witness or a hash collision using rewinding. The direct profile's three interactive screening exponents are about 320, 281.17 and 256.17 bits. The source establishes honest-verifier privacy under its independent sharing and hiding assumptions. Transferring those results to quantum witness extraction after three challenge messages requires further hypotheses. The generic multi-round QROM reduction has a factor near 2⁷⁷¹·⁴ at 2¹²⁸ queries, plus an ordering term. Even an illustrative 768-bit per-stage screen already exceeds the wire allowance for every party domain in these layouts. This quantifies a limitation of that proof technique. No corresponding forgery was observed or derived. A tighter result would need a quantum extractor for all adaptive broadcasts and a concrete mapping to the registered compiler. [Assumption and loss analysis](review/REVIEW.md) · [Feneuil and Rivain](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/1573) · [Don, Fehr and Majenz](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.05207)

The joint transaction retains four-limb funded commitments and nullifiers, ordered ML-KEM-1024/ChaCha20-Poly1305 receipts and the swap's existing joint digest and two SLH-DSA authorizations. The recipient must open and match its intended output before consenting. A proof of a receipt digest alone cannot ensure a malicious sender supplied a usable ciphertext. Fees, anchors, nullifiers, commitments, swap pairing, correlated retries and timing remain observable. A party that later obtains the long-lived receiving secret can open recorded receipts. Interactive witness privacy gives no multi-user key-privacy or adaptive sender/recipient composition theorem for this view. [Funded-receipt analysis](../spend-proof-decision/RECEIVING.md) · [Spend, collision and composition games](../amplified-spend/SECURITY.md)

**Next decision-changing test.** Test one versioned membership design using the Libert–Ling–Nguyen–Wang SIS-based binary accumulator and a hidden-leaf linkage to the monetary proof. The present two paths cost 120 of 164 Poseidon2 permutations per signed leg. Keeping the existing non-path monetary rows and broadcast schedule at 2¹⁶ parties, removal of both paths and terminal root links lowers the wire projection to 207,408 B. This leaves **33,676 B total** for two new private memberships, full 2⁴⁰ capacity, same-note equality and all binding to the branch, nullifier and transcript. That is an optimistic screening allowance. Price its actual dimensions, openings, quantum assumptions and migration proof before building a wallet. In particular, require a common hidden leaf opening across both proof systems and rule out duplicate spending during any authenticated dual-root transition. Changing an accumulator changes funded roots and cannot by itself close the Fiat–Shamir or receipt-composition gap. The devnet retains its registered roots. The bounds concern the two specified port layouts and commitment construction. A different wiring protocol or a tighter quantum reduction would need its own measurement and review.
