# Next priority: complete-payment proof-size gate

This experiment is NOT STARTED by the synchronization milestone. Finish its
independent acceptance and stop synchronization work first. Standalone PoW remains
the preferred direction; consensus integration does not follow from a wallet PASS.

The decision question is whether a materially different transparent commitment/
proof route can reduce an entire ordinary payment by at least10 times while
preserving the complete relation and an explicit, reviewed security profile.
The reference is the retained complete CP03 relation and observed1751265.75B body
mean. A175126B body is a researcher screening target, not an owner service promise
or sufficient justification for annual participation costs: even that magnitude
still implies roughly17.5TB/year per body copy at100M payments.

The first nominated candidate is the already preserved WHIR/sumcheck source under
`source/experiments/compact-challenge-v1/view01/{whir,sumcheck}`. It changes the
polynomial commitment/proximity machinery used by the direct-FRI reference.
Smaller complete proofs are a hypothesis, not a result or an architecture choice.
Its README describes a hiding PCS, which alone is not a complete private-payment
proof or a post-quantum composition theorem. Existing rejection/blocker records
must be reconciled first; do not reopen a closed attempt without a new, explicit
construction or verifier correspondence question.

The exact prior native disposition is preserved, with source hashes and verbatim
excerpts, in [PRIOR_DISPOSITION.json](PRIOR_DISPOSITION.json): route C of the
2026-11-17 `architecture-feasibility/proof-alternatives/DECISION.md` and the
independent `complete-driver/REVIEW.md`, lines 193–195. The DCH/Pack complete
successor aborted after 94.555658892 seconds in the lower-triangular rotation
accumulator and produced no proof. Sparse-before-commitment representation,
producer/verifier metadata bounds and target sampler/norm/entropy correspondence
remain explicit prerequisites for a native retry. This is a separate lattice
construction; its failure is not evidence against WHIR. The new WHIR question is
whether its exact hiding PCS can support the complete payment relation with a
qualified verifier and materially smaller complete bodies. Neither that mapping
nor its result is established by the preserved source tree.

The executable gate should:

1. Pin the exact source, construction variant, field/extension, parameters,
   verifier and primary security evidence. Resolve missing mandatory mechanisms
   and any applicable known attack before dependent integration. State remaining
   knowledge, hiding, Fiat–Shamir/QROM and receipt/proof composition qualifications.
2. Implement the same complete two-input/two-output ordinary-payment relation:
   secret-derived ownership/nullifiers, note commitments, both depth40 occurrence
   memberships and index bounds, exact bounded integer conservation/fee, zero
   ordinary credit/debit, canonical statement and full receipt/context binding.
   Receipt decryptability remains the same separate obligation as in CP03.
3. Create three fresh complete proofs with fresh private randomness, then verify
   from public artifacts in new processes. Test changed value, owner, membership,
   nullifier, receipt binding and malformed proof controls. A small PCS example,
   benchmark or omitted constraint is not this gate's result.
4. Measure complete body bytes, prover/verifier CPU, elapsed time, peak memory and
   public preprocessing separately. Predeclare bounded single-job allocations;
   use the retained4GiB research memory envelope as the starting comparison, not
   an inferred owner requirement. Do not lower security merely to meet size.
5. Obtain non-author reproduction and decide whether complete-proof/storage gains
   justify a later ledger/wallet integration milestone. A failed source/security
   gate or failed complete relation remains a precise unresolved result, never a
   generic production-readiness rejection or invented cryptographic guarantee.

Do not turn this into further wallet/CI optimization. Do not nominate a winner
because its library is available. If no complete candidate survives, the remaining
alternative is an explicitly costed full storage/availability/participation model,
including bootstrap and archive responsibilities. Owner acceptance of the current
175.126575TB/year first-copy model must not be inferred.
