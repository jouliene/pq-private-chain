# First local PoW devnet — acceptance evidence

**Decision.** Use direct, wallet-created APV2 singletons in the first local development network. Three peers run separate validation processes with their own SQLite databases, pending pools, verifier roles and block histories. Two search separate nonce spaces with ordinary CPUs. The fixed local target requires 22 leading zero bits of validated work. The node, miner and wallet implementation used for clean reproduction is `8c4fb94334994ac98253348384050f23904d40a5`, descended from `46932763d7a0e6d03ea848ae2e22ab65ef48d5df`. See [operator commands](README.md), [consensus rules](CONSENSUS.md), [independent review](review/REVIEW.md) and [hosted walkthrough](https://github.com/jouliene/pq-private-chain/actions/workflows/devnet.yml).

## Measured costs

On an Intel Core i7-8700K with 6 physical cores, four fresh ordinary payments in the controlled author walkthrough each occupied **230,206 bytes**, including both encrypted output sidecars. The selected chain contained 13 empty blocks of 222 B, one block of 230,432 B and one of 460,642 B, totaling **693,960 B**. One further independently authorized payment was mined only on the losing branch. Packet and block bytes exclude loopback transport framing. Each ordinary payment produces two public nullifiers including any circuit dummy. A count of six nullifiers represents three transactions in this history.

The fresh wallet-process path measured 13.42–13.70 s from the wallet's pay request to returned packet. This includes 4.54–4.70 s of local prover setup. Proof creation alone took 8.88–9.11 s wall and 17.29–17.71 CPU-s. Combined setup and proof used 24.53–25.05 CPU-s. Cumulative native process peak was 1,090,740–1,091,256 KiB (approximately 1.04 GiB). The isolated component verifier used about 10.4–10.5 ms CPU per singleton in its two measured checks. Full node admission also checks anchors, effects and persistent state. [Wallet meters](evidence/author/RESULT.json) · [Public verifier meters](crypto/evidence/WALLET_CHECK.json)

The second campaign used the default continuous miners throughout proving. Both miners returned ten solved proposals. The first wallet proof expired before submission. The second used a fresh H11 anchor, entered H18 and was observed with an H19 successor. The receiving wallet opened 17 units. For this intent, uploads totaled **460,458 application-frame bytes** over two TCP requests, including the expired attempt; replies added 161 B. The nineteen selected blocks gave eighteen inter-block observations: median **1.62 s**, mean **1.54 s**, range **0.15–2.99 s**. These timestamps are local miner observations. The manual walkthrough's median interval of 6.29 s includes test pauses and cannot estimate steady service. One successful live intent does not establish a service rate or a universal inclusion bound. ## Remaining obligations

- The chain archives each complete unaggregated payment. At 100M included payments/year, 230,206 B/payment alone gives **23.0206 decimal TB per first archival copy**; length framing adds 0.0004 TB and block headers add 222 B per mined block. Indexes, side chains, multiple copies, retransmissions, failed submissions and wallet recovery are additional. The 1 and 5 TB comparison scenarios remain unmet by this body encoding. No owner-approved annual storage or traffic ceiling is assumed.
- The APV2 proof and its Poseidon2-based note system retain their registered research assumptions. The receiving implementation uses ML-KEM-1024 and ChaCha20-Poly1305. The private prover keeps the inner witness within the wallet. Neither finite tests nor the choice of KEM establish complete quantum knowledge soundness, noninteractive zero knowledge or recipient anonymity for the composed protocol. See [previous security analysis](../experiments/advancing-payments/DECISION.md).
- Coinbases publicly identify the miner spending key and amount. Sparse activity leaks timing information. Nodes see loopback submissions. Wallets trust their selected local validating node and materialize common selected history when scanning; the earlier CP03 incremental-sync experiment remains a separate reference. Cold recovery needs the seed **and** available selected public history. This devnet supplies neither deployed network privacy nor pruning or bootstrap economics.
- Fixed easy PoW, an unbounded archival side-chain store, local topology, a 64-entry pool and an eight-height usable anchor window restrict the service claim to this research network. A lone payment can be submitted with no builder. Actual inclusion depends on PoW, propagation, current spentness and expiry.
- Keep CP03 and WHIR executable for comparison. Keep versioned native private asset transfers with native fees and all-or-nothing two-owner swaps on the roadmap. The global blockchain objective remains active. [Versioning and extension design](../experiments/wallet-created/EXTENSIONS.md)
